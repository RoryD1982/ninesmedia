Common Questions

FAQ

How much does digital marketing cost with Nines Media?

Every client is different, so we don't publish fixed rates. Pricing depends on the channels, ad spend, creative requirements and level of involvement that makes sense for your business. We'll give you a tailored quote after your free audit — no generic packages, no surprises.

How quickly will I see results?

Paid channels (Google Ads, Meta) typically show measurable results within 2–4 weeks. Organic channels (SEO, content) take 3–6 months to compound. We set clear benchmarks in your first 30 days and report weekly so you always know where you stand.

Do I own my ad accounts and creative assets?

Yes, always. You retain full ownership of your Google Ads account, Meta Business Manager, and all creative files we produce. We request access, we never own it. If you ever move on, everything stays with you.

What industries do you work with?

We work across retail, professional services, hospitality, property, education, and e-commerce — primarily in South Africa. We've built campaigns for local service businesses (R5k/month budgets) and national brands (R500k/month budgets). The strategies differ; the rigour doesn't.

What does the free digital audit include?

A senior strategist reviews your current website, paid ads, social presence, and competitor landscape. You get a written report covering your biggest growth levers, where your budget is being wasted, and a prioritised action plan — no strings attached. Claim yours here →

Can you work with our existing in-house team?

Absolutely. Many of our clients have internal marketing staff who handle content and community. We slot in as the strategy, media buying, or performance layer — or we run the whole engine. We'll scope it around how you actually work.
