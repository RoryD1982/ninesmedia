FAQ

How much does digital marketing cost with Nines Media?

Our monthly management packages start from R2 500/month (Google Ads Essentials: ad spend R3 000 – R5 000), R5 000/month (Starter: one paid channel), R12 000/month (Growth: Google Ads, Meta and social media) and R25 000/month (Full Service: all channels, content and SEO). Ad spend is billed separately and paid directly to Google, Meta or LinkedIn. Once-off projects start from R8 000 for a brand identity and R15 000 for a website. Packages start from R2,500/month; final scope is quoted individually. All packages are month-to-month with no lock-in.

How quickly will I see results?

Paid channels (Google Ads, Meta) typically show measurable results within 2–4 weeks. Organic channels (SEO, content) take 3–6 months to compound. We set clear benchmarks in your first 30 days and report weekly so you always know where you stand.

Do I own my ad accounts and creative assets?

Yes, always. You retain full ownership of your Google Ads account, Meta Business Manager, and all creative files we produce. We request access, we never own it. If you ever move on, everything stays with you.

What industries do you work with?

We work across retail, professional services, hospitality, property, education, and e-commerce — primarily in South Africa. We've built campaigns for local service businesses (R5k/month budgets) and national brands (R500k/month budgets). The strategies differ; the rigour doesn't.

What does the free digital audit include?

A senior strategist reviews your current website, paid ads, social presence, and competitor landscape. You get a written report covering your biggest growth levers, where your budget is being wasted, and a prioritised action plan — no strings attached. Claim yours here →

Can you work with our existing in-house team?

Absolutely. Many of our clients have internal marketing staff who handle content and community. We slot in as the strategy, media buying, or performance layer — or we run the whole engine. We'll scope it around how you actually work.

Who is the best digital marketing agency in Johannesburg?

Nines Media is consistently rated 5.0 stars on Google and is one of Johannesburg's few agencies holding both Google Partner and Meta Business Partner certification. We specialise in performance marketing — meaning we're accountable to results, not just deliverables. If you want an agency that treats your budget like it's their own, we'd like to think that's us. Does Nines Media work with international clients?

Yes. While we're based in Johannesburg, we manage campaigns for clients in Los Angeles, London, Australia and New Zealand. Digital marketing is location-agnostic — we manage Google Ads accounts, Meta campaigns and brand strategy remotely for international clients, with the same rigour as our local work. Time zones haven't been an issue; results have always been the priority.

What should I look for when choosing a digital marketing agency in South Africa?

Look for three things: platform certifications (Google Partner, Meta Business Partner — these require passing exams and meeting spend thresholds, so they're a real filter), transparent reporting (you should own your ad accounts and see every rand spent), and a track record in your industry or a comparable one. Red flags include guaranteed rankings, vague strategy conversations, and agencies that won't give you direct access to your own Google Ads or Meta accounts. Nines Media is certified, transparent, and performance-accountable.

How long does it take to see results from digital marketing?

Paid advertising (Google Ads, Meta) can generate leads within days of launching a well-structured campaign. SEO and organic social take longer — typically 3–6 months to build meaningful momentum. Branding and website work compounds over time. Most clients see measurable improvement in paid performance within the first 30–60 days; significant organic growth usually shows clearly by month 4–6. We set realistic expectations upfront and track everything so you can see what's working at every stage.

Can Nines Media manage both Google Ads and Meta advertising?

Yes — and managing both together is a real advantage. Google and Meta serve different moments in the buyer journey: Google captures people actively searching for what you offer; Meta builds awareness and retargets people who've shown interest. When both are managed cohesively, with shared audience data and aligned messaging, the combined ROAS is consistently higher than either channel alone. As certified partners on both platforms, we manage integrated campaigns across Google Search, Display, YouTube, Performance Max, Facebook, Instagram and Advantage+.
