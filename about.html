Who We Are

We Are
Nines
Media

We're a full-service digital marketing and creative agency based in Johannesburg, Gauteng — creating work that is entrenched in the lives of people.

We harness creativity, technology, and culture to build connected brands that drive real value. As a certified Google and META Partner, we combine AI-driven marketing, performance advertising — Google Ads, PPC, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn — with creative branding and content strategy to deliver results across South Africa.

We create work that brings brands closer to consumers, consumers closer to communities, and communities closer to the wider world. From Johannesburg and Cape Town to Los Angeles, London, Sydney and Auckland — we help brands grow wherever they are.

Our approach can answer just about any challenge your brand may have — whether you need a digital advertising partner, a creative agency, a social media management team, or a strategic marketing consultant. Nines Media is your single source for all of it.
