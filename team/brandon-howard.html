Brandon Howard is Head of Strategy at Nines Media, a certified Google Partner and Meta Business Partner digital marketing agency based at Dainfern Square in Johannesburg.

He plans and oversees client strategy across paid search, paid social and SEO — deciding which channels a business should invest in, setting up the tracking that proves what's working, and making sure campaigns are judged on leads and revenue rather than clicks. He also writes the guides on the Nines Media blog, covering what digital marketing costs in South Africa, how to run Google Ads and Meta campaigns profitably, and how to choose and work with an agency.
