Campaign Creative

META carousel ad — "Your Skin, Reimagined." 3-card sequence driving from awareness to product page. Targeted women 25–45 interested in clean beauty across South Africa.

The Problem

The brand had a great product and a loyal following of around 4,000 Instagram fans — but their paid media was non-existent and online sales had plateaued. They were spending money on content that wasn't converting, and had no visibility on which channels or messages were actually working.

The Solution

We audited their organic content, identified their three highest-converting product lines, and built a full-funnel META strategy: awareness carousel ads drove cold traffic, retargeting ads hit warm audiences with social proof and UGC, and Google Shopping captured high-intent search demand. Every rand spent was tracked to revenue.
