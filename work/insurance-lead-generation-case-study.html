The Client

A South African insurance brand selling directly to consumers. The client asked us not to name them, so this case study is anonymised. The results are real and are reported as the client measured them.

The Challenge

Insurance is one of the most competitive and expensive categories in paid search. The brand needed far more enquiries, but more volume alone wasn't the goal: the sales team needed leads they could actually close.

What We Did

  • Google Ads: campaigns built around high-intent insurance searches, so ads reached people actively looking for cover. See digital marketing for insurance.
  • Meta ads: Facebook and Instagram campaigns to reach likely buyers who weren't searching yet, with retargeting for people who had already shown interest.
  • Landing pages and conversion optimisation: focused pages and quote forms designed to turn more visitors into enquiries and to qualify them before they reached sales. More on conversion rate optimisation.
  • Lead tracking and CRM: every lead tracked from ad to outcome, so budget could follow the campaigns that produced sales, not just form fills.

Why the Closing Ratio Improved

Lead volume and lead quality often pull in opposite directions. Because every lead was tracked through to the sales outcome, we could see which searches, audiences and pages produced policyholders and shift budget towards them. That's how leads increased by 1,000% while the closing ratio also rose by 30 percentage points.

What This Means for Your Business

The same approach works beyond insurance: track leads all the way to revenue, put budget where customers come from, and fix the pages and forms in between. It's how we run performance marketing for every client, with packages from R2,500/month (see pricing).

Results relate to one client over a six-month period and depend on market, offer, budget and sales follow-up. They are not a guarantee of future results.
