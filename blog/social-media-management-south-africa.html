How Often Should You Post?

As a rough guide for South African brands: Instagram 4–5 feed posts plus regular Stories a week; Facebook 4–5 posts a week; LinkedIn 3–4 posts a week for B2B; TikTok as often as you can produce good video. Twelve posts a month is a minimum presence, not a growth plan — growth usually needs more frequent content, video, or paid support.

Organic Social vs Paid Social

Organic content builds your brand with people who already follow you. Paid social puts you in front of new people and is how most businesses turn social into leads and sales. The strongest results come from running both together — see our guide to paid social media advertising.

Questions to Ask Any Social Media Agency

  • Who creates the content — in-house designers or outsourced freelancers?
  • Do you produce video, or only static graphics?
  • How fast do you respond to comments and messages?
  • What do you report on, and how do you connect social to enquiries or sales?
  • Is ad spend included, or separate?

Frequently Asked Questions

What do social media management services include?

Typically a content strategy and calendar, content creation (design, video and copy), scheduling and posting, community management, and monthly reporting. Many agencies also manage paid social campaigns alongside organic content.

How much does social media management cost in South Africa?

Most South African agencies charge between R3,000 and R18,000 a month depending on platforms, posting frequency and video. At Nines Media, social media management is included in our Growth package from R12,000 a month, or quoted individually.

Which platforms should my business be on?

Be where your customers are. Most consumer brands start with Instagram and Facebook; B2B companies prioritise LinkedIn; TikTok suits brands targeting younger audiences and those that can produce regular short video.

Do you create the content or do I?

We do — design, copywriting and video are produced by our team. You approve the content calendar before anything is published.

Is ad spend included?

No. Paid social ad spend is billed separately and paid directly to Meta, LinkedIn or TikTok from accounts you own.
