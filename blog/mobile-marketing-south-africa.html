Mobile Marketing in South Africa: Strategies for a Mobile-First Market

By Brandon Howard2026-10-127 min read

South Africa is one of the most mobile-dependent internet markets in the world. Over 90% of South Africans access the internet primarily via smartphone. Data is expensive relative to income, and consumers are selective about what they engage with. A mobile-first marketing strategy isn't optional — it's the only strategy that works at scale.

The SA Mobile Market in 2026

  • Smartphone penetration: over 90% of internet users
  • WhatsApp: used by approximately 90% of SA smartphone users
  • Facebook: over 24 million monthly active users
  • Average daily screen time: 4+ hours (higher than UK and USA)
  • Mobile data costs: declining but still a consideration for rural and lower-income markets

Mobile Website Optimisation

If your website doesn't load fast and look good on a phone, you're losing the majority of your potential customers before they even read your content. Key requirements:

  • Page load time under 3 seconds on mobile (test with Google PageSpeed Insights)
  • Tap targets (buttons, links) at least 44px tall
  • No horizontal scrolling
  • Click-to-call phone numbers
  • WhatsApp contact button
  • Forms with minimal fields and keyboard-appropriate input types

SMS Marketing in South Africa

SMS marketing remains effective in South Africa, particularly for time-sensitive promotions and appointment reminders. Open rates consistently exceed 90%. Key rules under POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act): you must have consent to send marketing SMS, and every message must include an opt-out mechanism. Platforms like BulkSMS, Clickatell, and SMSPortal are widely used in SA.

WhatsApp Business Marketing

WhatsApp Business allows SA businesses to set up automated replies, broadcast lists, and product catalogues. The WhatsApp Business API (for larger businesses) enables CRM integration and automated customer journeys. WhatsApp is where SA consumers are most active and most responsive — building your marketing presence there is increasingly non-negotiable.

Mobile Google Ads

Over 70% of Google searches in South Africa are made on mobile. Google Ads campaigns should be structured with mobile-specific bid adjustments, call extensions (so users can call directly from the ad), and landing pages tested extensively on mobile before launch.

POPIA compliance: All direct marketing in South Africa — SMS, email, WhatsApp — requires prior consent under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Maintain a record of how and when each contact gave consent. The Information Regulator can impose fines of up to R10 million for non-compliance.
