Why Work With a Google Partner Agency

Google Partner status means our team holds current Google Ads certifications and the accounts we manage meet Google's performance and spend requirements. In practice, it gives you a team that keeps up with how the platform changes — and access to Google's partner support when something goes wrong.

Beyond the badge, here is what you get from us:

  • Conversion tracking first. We don't launch until calls, forms and sales are being measured, because bidding without data is guesswork.
  • Weekly optimisation. Search terms, bids, budgets and ads are reviewed every week.
  • Plain-language reporting. Leads, cost per lead and revenue, plus what we changed and why.
  • Your account, your data. Everything is set up in your name. You keep full access, always.
  • A fixed fee. We don't take a percentage of your ad spend, so we're never rewarded just for spending more.

How We Run Your Google Ads

  1. Free audit. We review your current account or market, your website and competitors, and send a written report with a recommended budget and quote.
  2. Tracking and structure. GA4, Google Ads conversions and call tracking are set up; campaigns are built or rebuilt around your most profitable services.
  3. Launch. Campaigns go live with close daily monitoring through the learning period.
  4. Optimise and scale. We cut waste, test new ads and landing pages, and move budget to what produces customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Google Ads agency do?

A Google Ads agency plans, builds and manages your campaigns: keyword research, ad copy, bidding, budgets, conversion tracking, landing page recommendations, testing and reporting. A good agency optimises weekly and reports on leads and revenue rather than clicks.

How much does Google Ads management cost in South Africa?

Management fees in South Africa typically range from R2,500 to R15,000 a month. Nines Media packages start from R4,000 a month for Google Ads only (ad spend up to R5,000). Your ad spend is separate and is paid directly to Google.

Is Nines Media a Google Partner?

Yes. Nines Media is a certified Google Partner agency, and also a Meta Business Partner.

Can you take over my existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We audit the account first, keep the history and data intact, and restructure campaigns where needed rather than starting from scratch.

How long before Google Ads generates leads?

Search campaigns can produce enquiries within the first week. The first four to six weeks are a learning period; cost per lead usually improves from the second month as we optimise.

Do you lock clients into contracts?

No. Our Google Ads management is month-to-month, and the ad account stays in your name.
