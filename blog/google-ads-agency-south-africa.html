Google Ads Agency South Africa: What to Expect and How to Choose

By Nines Media2026-10-128 min read

Google Ads is the fastest way to get qualified leads for your South African business. But without the right agency managing your campaigns, it's also the fastest way to burn through your budget without results. Here's what to look for — and what to avoid.

What a Google Ads Agency Does

A Google Ads agency builds, manages, and optimises your paid search campaigns. This includes keyword research, ad copywriting, bid management, audience targeting, conversion tracking setup, landing page recommendations, and regular performance reporting.

The difference between a good agency and a bad one isn't just technical skill — it's accountability. The best agencies own your results, not just your tasks.

Google Ads Campaign Types in 2026

  • Search: Text ads triggered by specific keyword searches — highest intent, best for lead generation
  • Performance Max: AI-driven campaigns across all Google inventory — effective but requires careful management
  • Display: Visual banner ads across the Google Display Network — good for retargeting and awareness
  • Shopping: Product listing ads for e-commerce
  • YouTube: Video ads — strong for brand building and remarketing

Warning: Be cautious of agencies that charge a % of ad spend with no performance accountability. Their incentive is to increase your budget, not improve your results. Flat-fee or hybrid models with performance KPIs align incentives better.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  1. Will I have direct access to my Google Ads account at all times?
  2. How do you set up conversion tracking?
  3. What's your reporting cadence and format?
  4. Can you share examples of campaigns in my industry?
  5. What happens if we don't hit targets?

Green Flags in a Google Ads Agency

  • Google Partner or Premier Partner status
  • Builds conversion tracking before launching campaigns
  • Recommends landing page improvements, not just ads
  • Reports in leads and cost-per-lead, not just clicks and impressions
