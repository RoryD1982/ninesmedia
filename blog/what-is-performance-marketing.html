What Is Performance Marketing? A Plain-English Guide

Nines Media Team Updated 9 October 2026 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Performance marketing means paying for, and optimising towards, measurable actions — leads, sales, bookings or app installs.
  • Typical channels: Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, affiliates and email — anywhere results can be tracked.
  • Core metrics: CPA (cost per acquisition), ROAS (return on ad spend), conversion rate and customer lifetime value.
  • It only works with accurate tracking and a clear view of what a customer is worth.

"Performance marketing" gets used loosely, but the idea is simple: you measure marketing by the results it produces, and you spend more on what works and less on what doesn't. For South African businesses watching every rand, it's one of the most practical ways to approach growth.

What counts as performance marketing?

Any channel where you can connect spend to a specific outcome:

  • Search ads — Google Ads and Microsoft Ads.
  • Social ads — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn.
  • Shopping and marketplace ads — Google Shopping, Takealot sponsored products.
  • Affiliate and partner marketing — paying commission per sale or lead.
  • Email and SMS — measured by revenue per send.
  • Programmatic and video — when optimised and measured against conversions.

The key metrics

Metric What it means Example
CPC Cost per click R10,000 spend ÷ 1,000 clicks = R10
Conversion rate % of visitors who take action 50 leads ÷ 1,000 clicks = 5%
CPL / CPA Cost per lead or acquisition R10,000 ÷ 50 = R200 per lead
ROAS Revenue ÷ ad spend R60,000 revenue ÷ R10,000 = 6:1
CAC Total cost to acquire a customer, including fees (R10,000 + R5,000 fees) ÷ 10 customers = R1,500
LTV Revenue or profit from a customer over time Average customer spends R12,000 over 2 years

Work out what a customer is worth first

Performance marketing depends on knowing your numbers:

  1. Average order or contract value.
  2. Gross margin.
  3. Repeat purchase rate or customer lifetime.
  4. Lead-to-customer rate (for service businesses).

From that you can calculate the maximum you can afford to pay per lead or sale — your target CPA — and your break-even ROAS (1 ÷ gross margin). A 40% margin means a break-even ROAS of 2.5:1.

Tracking is the foundation

Without accurate tracking, "performance" marketing is guesswork. You need:

  • Conversion tracking on every platform (Google tag, Meta Pixel and Conversions API, TikTok and LinkedIn tags).
  • GA4 configured with key events.
  • Call and WhatsApp tracking.
  • CRM integration so you can see which leads became customers.
  • Consistent UTM tags on every link.

How performance campaigns are optimised

  • Bids and budgets move towards campaigns with the best CPA or ROAS.
  • Creative testing finds the messages that convert.
  • Landing page testing raises conversion rates.
  • Audience and keyword refinement removes waste.
  • Offline conversion feedback teaches platforms which leads are valuable.

Performance vs brand: you need both

Purely chasing short-term conversions eventually hits a ceiling — you run out of people already ready to buy. Brand-building widens the pool, makes ads more effective and lowers acquisition costs over time. A practical split for many SMEs is to keep most of the budget on performance while reserving a portion for brand-building content and awareness campaigns.

Common mistakes

  • Optimising for cheap leads that never buy.
  • Trusting each platform's self-reported conversions without checking the CRM.
  • Ignoring margins — high revenue at low ROAS can lose money.
  • Changing campaigns daily before they've gathered data.

Frequently asked questions

What is performance marketing in simple terms?

It's marketing where every campaign is measured by a concrete business result — a lead, sale or booking — and budget moves towards whatever produces those results most cost-effectively.

What is the difference between performance marketing and brand marketing?

Performance marketing targets immediate, measurable actions. Brand marketing builds awareness and preference over time. They work best together: brand makes performance cheaper, and performance funds growth.

What is a good ROAS?

It depends on your margins. A business with 50% gross margin needs at least 2:1 ROAS to break even on ad spend, while a low-margin retailer may need 5:1 or more. Calculate your break-even ROAS before setting targets.

What does a performance marketing agency do?

It plans, runs and optimises paid campaigns against business targets such as cost per lead or ROAS, sets up tracking, tests creative and landing pages, and reports on results tied to revenue.

Is performance marketing suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Because spend is flexible and measurable, small businesses can start with one channel, prove a cost per customer, and scale what works.
