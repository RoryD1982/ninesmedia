How to Spend R10,000 a Month on Digital Marketing

By Brandon Howard 11 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • At R10,000 a month you can run one paid channel properly managed with a meaningful ad budget.
  • Put most of the money into ad spend; management should never be the bigger share.
  • Prove one channel works before splitting the budget across two.

R10,000 a month is a common budget for South African small businesses that have outgrown DIY marketing. It's enough to run one channel properly with professional management, but not enough to do everything at once. Here's how to make it count.

Before You Spend: The Free Foundations

  • A complete Google Business Profile with photos, services and recent reviews.
  • A fast, mobile-friendly website with your phone number and WhatsApp visible.
  • Conversion tracking for calls, forms and WhatsApp clicks.
  • Someone who replies to enquiries within the hour.

Three Sensible Ways to Split R10,000

ApproachExample splitBest for
Managed Google Ads~R5,000 management + ~R5,000 Google AdsService businesses where customers search
Managed Meta~R5,000 management incl. creative + ~R5,000 Meta adsVisual, consumer and local lifestyle businesses
Essentials + more ad spend~R2,500 Google Ads management + ~R5,000 ads + ~R2,500 for content or photosBusinesses that want maximum spend on clicks

These are examples. Your best split depends on click costs in your industry and how your customers buy. See Google Ads costs and Facebook ads costs in South Africa.

Google or Meta?

  • Choose Google Ads if people search for what you sell: trades, professional services, clinics, B2B and urgent needs.
  • Choose Meta if people need to discover you: restaurants, fashion, beauty, events and new products.

Don't split R10,000 across both at the start. Each channel needs enough data to learn which people convert.

What R10,000 Can Realistically Achieve

As a rough guide, if clicks in your industry cost R15–R30, R5,000 in Google Ads buys around 165–330 clicks a month. If your landing page converts 5–8% of visitors, that's roughly 8–25 enquiries a month. Higher-value sectors such as legal and finance pay more per click, but each customer is usually worth more. These figures are illustrative; your results depend on your market, offer and website.

Managed or DIY?

Running ads yourself puts the full R10,000 into reach, but it costs your time and the learning curve can waste budget. Managed campaigns cost a fee, but you get tracking, optimisation and reporting done properly. Many small businesses start with an affordable digital agency package and take over later once they understand what works, or keep the agency as they grow.

When to Add a Second Channel

Add a second channel when the first produces customers at a cost you're happy with for two to three months in a row. At that point a move to roughly R15,000 a month lets you run search and social together. If you're starting smaller, see the R5,000 plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is R10,000 a month enough for digital marketing?

Yes, for one properly managed paid channel with a meaningful ad budget, alongside free foundations such as a Google Business Profile and good lead follow-up.

How should I split R10,000 between management and ads?

Aim for at least half in ad spend. A common split is about R5,000 management and R5,000 ad spend, or R2,500 management with more left for ads.

Should I use Google Ads or Facebook ads with R10,000?

Use Google Ads if customers search for what you sell, and Meta if they need to discover you. Start with one before adding the other.
