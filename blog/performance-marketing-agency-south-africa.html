Performance marketing means paying for advertising that you can measure all the way to a lead or a sale, then putting more money behind what works and less behind what doesn't. As a certified Google Partner and Meta Business Partner, we run it for South African businesses that want every rand of ad spend to be accountable. (New to the term? Read what performance marketing is.)

Leads, cost per lead, cost per sale and return on ad spend in plain language, with what we're changing next.

How We Run Performance Campaigns

  1. Work out what a customer is worth. Before spending anything, we agree your average sale value, margin and close rate, so we know the most you can afford to pay for a lead.
  2. Set up tracking first. Forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks and purchases are tracked before campaigns go live. Without this, optimisation is guesswork. Our conversion tracking guide explains what we set up.
  3. Launch on the highest-intent channel. Usually Google Search for service businesses, or Meta for visual and impulse products.
  4. Optimise every week. Negative keywords, bid and budget shifts, new ad tests and audience changes, driven by which leads actually become customers.
  5. Scale what pays back. Once cost per lead is below target, we add budget or a second channel. If it isn't, we say so and fix the offer, page or targeting first.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a performance marketing agency?

A performance marketing agency runs paid advertising, mainly Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), and judges its work by measurable outcomes such as leads, sales, cost per lead and return on ad spend rather than by reach or impressions.

How much does a performance marketing agency cost in South Africa?

At Nines Media, management starts from R2,500/month for Google Ads only on small budgets, R5,000/month for one channel, and R12,000/month for Google Ads plus Meta. Final scope is quoted individually. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to Google or Meta.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. Our management fee is fixed, so it doesn't grow just because your ad budget does. Ad spend is paid straight to the platforms from accounts in your name.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can guarantee a specific number of leads or sales, because results depend on your market, offer, website and how quickly you follow up. What we commit to is proper tracking, transparent reporting and acting on the data every month.

How long before performance campaigns pay off?

Search campaigns usually produce leads in the first few weeks. The first one to three months are a learning period while we cut wasted spend and find the best keywords, audiences and ads, so judge results on months two to three rather than week one.

What's the difference between performance marketing and digital marketing?

Digital marketing is the broad umbrella, including SEO, social media, content and branding. Performance marketing is the paid, measurable part of it, where you pay for clicks, leads or sales and optimise to a cost-per-result target.

Is there a contract?

No. All packages are month-to-month, and you own your ad accounts, tracking and data.
