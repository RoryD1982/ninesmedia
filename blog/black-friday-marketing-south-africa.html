Black Friday Marketing in South Africa: A Planning Checklist

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday 27 November, with Cyber Monday on 30 November.
  • Build your email, WhatsApp and remarketing audiences before November — ad costs rise sharply.
  • Make sure discounts are genuine: misleading pricing can breach consumer law.

Black Friday has become one of South Africa's biggest retail periods, with many shoppers planning purchases weeks in advance. In 2026, Black Friday falls on Friday 27 November and Cyber Monday on 30 November. Here's how to prepare.

Timeline

WhenWhat to do
Early NovemberFinalise offers, check stock, brief delivery partners, test website speed and checkout
Weeks beforeGrow email and WhatsApp lists with "early access" sign-ups; build remarketing audiences
Week beforeTeasers to your list; prepare ads and creative; schedule emails
Black Friday weekendLaunch offers, monitor stock and site, respond to messages fast
Cyber MondayOnline-focused offers; last-chance messages
Early DecemberFestive gifting campaigns; delivery cut-off reminders

Plan Genuine Offers

Choose offers you can afford: margin-safe discounts, bundles, free delivery thresholds or gifts with purchase. Make sure "was" prices are genuine. The Consumer Protection Act prohibits false, misleading or deceptive representations about prices, so inflated reference prices create legal and reputational risk.

Get Your Website Ready

  • Test speed on mobile data — see website speed.
  • Check checkout and payment options work smoothly.
  • Show delivery costs and timelines clearly.
  • Create a dedicated Black Friday landing page.
  • Make sure your hosting can handle spikes.

Use Your Own Audiences

Ad costs rise sharply as competition increases in late November. Your email list, WhatsApp subscribers (with consent) and social followers are your cheapest channel — build them early. See email marketing and POPIA and marketing.

Paid Ads

  • Increase budgets on proven campaigns rather than launching untested ones.
  • Use remarketing to reach people who viewed products recently.
  • Expect higher costs per click and per thousand impressions; set ROAS targets accordingly.
  • Make sure your product feed reflects sale prices accurately — see Google Ads for e-commerce.

Service Businesses Can Benefit Too

Gift vouchers, prepaid packages, annual plans and early-booking offers for the new year work well for salons, gyms, studios and professional services.

After the Weekend

Thank new customers, ask for reviews and invite them to join your list. Review what sold and what each channel cost, and note lessons for next year.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday 2026 in South Africa?

Black Friday 2026 is on Friday 27 November, and Cyber Monday is on Monday 30 November.

When should I start Black Friday marketing?

Start in early November or earlier: finalise offers, test your website, and grow email and WhatsApp lists before ad costs rise.

Are Black Friday discounts regulated in South Africa?

The Consumer Protection Act prohibits misleading representations about prices, so 'was' prices and discounts should be genuine.
