Google Analytics for South African Businesses: A Setup and Use Guide

By Brandon Howard2026-10-128 min read

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is the free website analytics tool every South African business with a website should be using. It tells you how many people visit your site, where they came from, what they did, and whether they became a lead or customer. Without it, you're making marketing decisions blind.

GA4 vs Universal Analytics: What Changed

Google replaced Universal Analytics (UA) with GA4 in 2023. If you haven't migrated or set up GA4, you have no historical data accumulating. Key differences:

  • GA4 is event-based rather than session-based — every user action (scroll, click, form submit) is an "event"
  • Better cross-device tracking — follows a user across mobile, tablet, and desktop
  • More powerful exploration and funnel reporting
  • Built-in AI insights and anomaly detection
  • Deeper Google Ads integration for conversion tracking

Essential GA4 Setup Steps for SA Businesses

  1. Create a GA4 property at analytics.google.com and add your website
  2. Install the tracking code via Google Tag Manager (recommended) or directly in your site's HTML
  3. Set up conversion events — form submissions, phone clicks, booking completions, purchases
  4. Link to Google Ads so ad conversions are tracked accurately
  5. Link to Google Search Console for organic search data
  6. Set up basic filters to exclude your own IP from data

The Metrics That Matter for SA Service Businesses

MetricWhat It Tells You
Users / SessionsHow much traffic your site receives
Conversion rateWhat % of visitors take a desired action
Traffic sourceWhether traffic comes from Google, social, direct, or referral
Landing page performanceWhich pages drive the most conversions
Device splitMobile vs desktop — critical for SA's mobile-first market
Bounce rate / engagement rateWhether visitors find what they're looking for

Using GA4 to Improve Your Marketing

The real value of GA4 isn't the data itself — it's the decisions it drives. Common insights for SA businesses:

  • "70% of our traffic is mobile but our mobile conversion rate is half our desktop rate" → fix the mobile experience
  • "Our Google Ads traffic has a 5% conversion rate but our organic traffic converts at 2%" → invest more in paid
  • "Our services page gets high traffic but almost no conversions" → rewrite the page with a clearer CTA
Common mistake: Many SA businesses install GA4 but never configure conversion events. Without conversions tracked, you can see traffic but not results. Set up at minimum: form submission event, click-to-call event, and for e-commerce, purchase event.

Track the Actions That Matter

GA4 records page views automatically, but the actions that matter to your business need to be marked as key events (conversions):

  • Contact and quote form submissions — tracked on the thank-you page or a confirmed submission, not just a button click.
  • Phone number taps and WhatsApp button clicks.
  • Bookings, purchases and their value.
  • Brochure or price list downloads.

Then import those key events into Google Ads so campaigns optimise for real enquiries.

Connect Your Google Tools

  • Google Ads — see campaign performance in GA4 and import conversions.
  • Search Console — see which searches bring organic visitors to each page.
  • Google Tag Manager — manage tracking without editing website code each time.

Consent and POPIA

Analytics and advertising cookies collect personal information. Tell visitors what you collect and why in a clear privacy notice, and consider a cookie consent banner — especially if you have international visitors or use advertising features like remarketing. Google's Consent Mode lets tags respect visitors' choices.

Common GA4 Mistakes

  • Leaving the default two-month data retention — change it to 14 months in Admin settings.
  • Not excluding internal traffic from your own office.
  • Counting every form button click as a lead, including failed submissions.
  • Never looking at the data. Set up a simple monthly report or Looker Studio dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Analytics free?

Yes. Standard GA4 is free. Google Analytics 360 is a paid enterprise version for very large organisations.

What should a South African business track in GA4?

Form submissions, phone and WhatsApp clicks, bookings and purchases, marked as key events, plus traffic sources and landing page performance.

Does Google Analytics comply with POPIA?

It can be used compliantly if you explain your data collection in a privacy notice, limit what you collect, and respect visitors' choices, for example through consent tools and Google's Consent Mode.
