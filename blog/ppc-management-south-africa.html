Pay-per-click advertising is the quickest way to put your business in front of people who are already searching for what you sell. It is also the quickest way to waste money if nobody is watching the account. Our PPC management service exists to make sure every rand of your budget is pointed at searches and audiences that actually buy.

What Our PPC Management Includes

Account audit & strategy

We start by reviewing your existing account (or market, if you're new), your competitors and your margins, then agree a target cost per lead or return on ad spend.

Conversion tracking

Google Ads conversions, GA4, call tracking and form tracking set up before we spend anything, so optimisation is based on real enquiries and sales.

Keyword & audience build

Tightly themed campaigns, match types that fit your budget, and a negative keyword list that keeps irrelevant searches out from day one.

Ad copy & creative

Search ads written for South African searchers, plus image and video creative for Display, YouTube, Performance Max and Meta where it makes sense.

Ongoing optimisation

Search-term reviews, bid strategy changes, budget shifts between campaigns and A/B tests on ads and landing pages — every week, not once a quarter.

Reporting you can read

Leads, cost per lead, sales and return on ad spend, in plain language, with what we changed and what we're testing next.

PPC Channels We Manage

  • Google Search — text ads on high-intent searches; usually the best starting point for lead generation. See our Google Ads management service.
  • Performance Max & Google Shopping — for e-commerce stores selling products online. More in our Google Shopping guide.
  • Display & YouTube — remarketing and awareness for businesses with longer buying cycles.
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram) — paid social for demand creation, lead forms and retargeting. See Facebook & Meta ads management.
  • LinkedIn — B2B lead generation on our Full Service package.

How We Manage Your PPC Campaigns

  1. Free audit (week 0). A senior strategist reviews your account, website and competitors and sends a written report with a quote.
  2. Tracking & build (weeks 1–2). We fix or install conversion tracking, restructure or build campaigns, and write the ads.
  3. Launch & learn (weeks 3–6). Budgets go live with tight daily monitoring while bidding algorithms gather data.
  4. Optimise & scale (month 2 onwards). We cut what doesn't convert, put more budget behind what does, and test new ads and landing pages.

Flat Fee, Not a Cut of Your Budget

Many PPC agencies charge a percentage of ad spend, which means their fee rises every time your budget does — whether results improve or not. We charge a fixed monthly management fee agreed upfront, so our incentive is to make your existing budget work harder, not to persuade you to spend more.

Who Our PPC Service Is For

  • Service businesses that need a steady flow of calls and enquiries — professional services, medical practices, trades, property, insurance and finance.
  • E-commerce stores that want profitable sales from Google Shopping and Meta.
  • B2B companies that need qualified leads, not just traffic.
  • Businesses already running ads in-house or with another agency that suspect money is being wasted.

If your budget is under about R3,000 a month, we'll tell you honestly whether paid search makes sense yet, or whether local SEO and your Google Business Profile are the better first step.

What to Expect From Good PPC Management

Paid search can produce enquiries in the first week, but the first four to six weeks are mostly about learning: which keywords convert, what a lead really costs, and which ads win. By month two or three you should see cost per lead falling and lead quality improving. Our guide to getting leads from Google Ads covers the playbook in detail.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PPC management?

PPC management is the ongoing work of planning, building, monitoring and optimising pay-per-click campaigns on platforms like Google Ads and Meta: keyword and audience research, ad copy, bidding, budgets, conversion tracking, testing and reporting.

How much does PPC management cost in South Africa?

Most South African agencies charge between R2,500 and R15,000 a month in management fees, depending on spend and channels. Nines Media packages start from R4,000 a month (Google Ads Essentials), with ad spend billed separately and paid directly to the platforms.

Do I need a minimum ad budget?

There is no fixed minimum, but most service businesses need at least R3,000 to R5,000 a month in ad spend to gather enough data to optimise. We'll recommend a budget based on your market's click costs during the free audit.

Do I own my Google Ads account?

Yes. Your ad accounts are set up in your name and you keep full access at all times. If you ever leave, the account and its history stay with you.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Our packages are month-to-month — we earn the next month by delivering results.

How quickly will I see results from PPC?

Ads can generate clicks and enquiries within days of launch. Expect the first four to six weeks to be a learning phase, with cost per lead typically improving from month two as we cut waste and scale what converts.
