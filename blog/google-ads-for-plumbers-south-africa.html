Google Ads for Plumbers in South Africa: Get More Calls Today

By Brandon Howard2026-10-126 min read

Plumbing is one of the best categories for Google Ads in South Africa. When a pipe bursts or a geyser fails, people search immediately and call whoever appears first. If you're not on page one, you don't exist. Here's how to change that.

Why Google Ads Works for Plumbers

Plumbing searches are high-intent and time-sensitive. Someone searching "emergency plumber Johannesburg" or "geyser replacement Cape Town" needs someone now. They're not comparing prices extensively — they're calling the first credible result. Google Ads puts you there immediately, without waiting months for SEO to kick in.

Best Keywords for Plumbing Companies

  • "emergency plumber [city]"
  • "plumber near me"
  • "geyser replacement [city]"
  • "blocked drain [city]"
  • "burst pipe repair [suburb]"
  • "plumbing company [city]"

Bid on suburb-level keywords (e.g. "plumber Randburg") where you can — they often have lower CPCs and higher intent than city-level terms.

Campaign Setup Essentials

  • Call extensions: Your phone number must be visible in every ad — most plumbing leads come through calls, not form fills
  • Location targeting: Limit your campaign to the suburbs and areas you actually service
  • Ad scheduling: If you don't offer 24/7 service, pause your ads outside business hours — you're wasting spend on calls you can't answer
  • Negative keywords: Add "DIY", "how to", "training", "courses" as negatives to avoid irrelevant clicks

Call Ads vs Search Ads for Plumbers

Most plumbing customers want to talk to someone straight away. Two campaign types matter:

  • Call ads (call-only) show your number and dial it directly on mobile. They suit emergencies — burst pipes, geysers, blocked drains — where people won't browse a website.
  • Standard search ads send people to a landing page. They suit planned work like bathroom renovations, geyser upgrades or solar geyser installations, where customers compare options.

Many plumbers run both: call ads for emergency keywords during working hours, and search ads for planned jobs.

What Your Landing Page Needs

  • Your phone number and a WhatsApp button at the top, visible without scrolling on a phone.
  • The areas you cover, listed by suburb.
  • Call-out fee or "free quote" policy, stated plainly — hidden pricing is a common reason people hang up.
  • Proof you're legitimate: registration (for example PIRB), insurance, years in business and recent Google reviews.
  • Photos of real jobs and your branded vehicles, not stock images.

Mistakes That Waste Plumbing Ad Budgets

  • Targeting a whole city when you only serve part of it. A Johannesburg South plumber paying for clicks from Midrand is losing money on every one.
  • Not tracking calls. Without call tracking you can't tell which keywords produce jobs, so you can't cut the ones that don't.
  • Missing calls. If ads run while nobody answers the phone, you're paying for leads that go to the next plumber. Schedule ads around when you can actually respond, or use a call answering service.
  • Ignoring negative keywords. Searches for plumbing jobs, apprenticeships, courses, parts prices and DIY guides all burn budget.

Google Ads and Your Google Business Profile

Many "plumber near me" searches are answered by the Google Maps results, not ads. Keep your Google Business Profile complete, add photos weekly and ask every satisfied customer for a review. Frequently Asked Questions

How much should a plumber spend on Google Ads in South Africa?

Most plumbing businesses spend between R3,000 and R12,000 a month on ads, depending on the city and how many areas they cover. Johannesburg and Cape Town are more competitive than smaller towns.

Are call ads better than search ads for plumbers?

For emergency work, call ads usually perform better because customers want to phone immediately. For planned jobs like renovations and geyser upgrades, search ads with a landing page work better.

How quickly can Google Ads get a plumber more calls?

Calls can start within days of launching. The first few weeks are about learning which keywords and areas produce real jobs, then cutting the rest.
