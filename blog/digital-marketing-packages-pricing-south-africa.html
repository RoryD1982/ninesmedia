Digital Marketing Packages & Pricing in South Africa (2026 Guide)

Nines Media Team Updated 9 October 2026 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • Most South African SMEs pay R5,000–R25,000 per month in agency fees for digital marketing, with ad spend paid to Google or Meta on top.
  • Entry packages (R3,000–R8,000) usually cover one channel. Multi-channel growth packages sit around R8,000–R25,000. Full-service retainers start near R25,000.
  • Always separate the management fee from the ad spend — the second goes to the platform, not the agency.
  • Nines Media packages start from R5,000/month, but every engagement is quoted after a free audit rather than sold off a fixed menu.

"How much does digital marketing cost?" is the first question almost every South African business owner asks — and the answer they usually get is "it depends". That is true, but it is not helpful. This guide gives you the actual ranges we see across the South African market in 2026, what you should get at each level, and how to tell whether a package is good value.

What does a digital marketing package include?

A "digital marketing package" is a monthly bundle of services. The most common components are:

  • Paid media management — Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, LinkedIn, TikTok or YouTube campaigns.
  • Social media management — content creation, posting, and community management.
  • SEO — technical fixes, on-page optimisation, content and link building.
  • Creative — ad designs, video, copywriting.
  • Reporting and strategy — dashboards, monthly reviews, budget recommendations.
  • Web and landing pages — usually a separate project fee, sometimes bundled as ongoing optimisation.

The price of any package is driven by three things: how many channels are included, how much original creative is produced, and how senior the people working on your account are.

Agency fee vs ad spend: the most important distinction

Your total investment has two parts:

  1. The management fee — what you pay the agency for strategy, setup, creative, optimisation and reporting.
  2. The media budget (ad spend) — what you pay Google, Meta or LinkedIn for clicks and impressions.

A business paying R10,000 a month "for Google Ads" might be paying R4,000 in fees and R6,000 in ad spend — or R10,000 in fees with almost nothing reaching Google. Always ask for the split in writing, and make sure ad spend is billed to a card or account in your name.

As a starting point for lead generation in South Africa, budget at least R5,000–R10,000 per month in ad spend per channel. Less than that and the platforms rarely gather enough data to optimise. Our guides on what Google Ads costs and what Facebook ads cost break this down by industry.

How agencies structure their pricing

  • Flat monthly retainer — the most common model in South Africa. Predictable, and it aligns the agency with getting more out of a fixed budget.
  • Percentage of ad spend — typically 10–20%. Common with larger budgets, but it rewards the agency for spending more, not for spending better.
  • Hybrid — a base retainer plus a smaller percentage above a spend threshold.
  • Project fees — for once-off work such as a website, brand identity or strategy sprint.
  • Performance-based — payment per lead or sale. Rare, and usually priced high to cover the agency's risk.

What you should get at each price point

R3,000–R8,000 per month

Expect a single channel run competently: campaign setup, a few ads or posts, and a monthly report. Do not expect original video, landing pages or strategic input. At this level, ask exactly how many hours per month go into your account — it is often three to six.

R8,000–R25,000 per month

This is where most South African SMEs get real results. You should receive a written strategy, two or three channels working together (for example Google Search for demand capture and Meta for retargeting), new creative every month, conversion tracking that ties spend to leads, and a monthly review call.

R25,000 per month and above

Full-service retainers should include a dedicated account lead, a creative team producing video and design, SEO and content, dashboard reporting across every channel, and quarterly planning tied to commercial targets.

Red flags in cheap packages

  • "Guaranteed page one on Google." No one can guarantee organic rankings.
  • Ad accounts in the agency's name. If you leave, you lose your data and history.
  • Vanity reporting. Likes, reach and impressions without leads, sales or cost per acquisition.
  • Long lock-in contracts before any results are proven.
  • No conversion tracking. If they cannot tell you cost per lead, they are not managing performance.

How to choose the right package

  1. Start with the goal, not the channel. More leads, more online sales and more awareness each need a different mix.
  2. Work back from cost per customer. If a new client is worth R20,000 to you, spending R2,000 to win one is excellent value.
  3. Fund one channel properly before adding a second. A well-run Google Search campaign beats four under-funded channels.
  4. Insist on tracking from day one. You should know your cost per lead within the first month.
  5. Review at 90 days. Paid channels should show measurable results inside three months; SEO takes longer (see our timeline guide).

Frequently asked questions

How much does digital marketing cost per month in South Africa?

For a small to medium business, expect R5,000–R25,000 per month in agency fees, plus a separate ad budget (often R5,000–R30,000) paid directly to Google, Meta or LinkedIn. Freelancers can be cheaper; national brands commonly spend R50,000+ a month on fees alone.

Are digital marketing packages better than a custom quote?

Packages are useful for comparing price levels, but a custom quote is usually better value because it matches channels and budget to your goals. A tiler in Fourways and an e-commerce brand selling nationally should not be on the same package.

Is ad spend included in digital marketing packages?

Usually not. Reputable agencies bill their management fee separately and let you pay Google and Meta directly from accounts you own. Be cautious of packages that bundle a vague 'ad budget' into one price — you cannot see how much is actually reaching the platform.

What is the minimum budget for digital marketing in South Africa?

Around R10,000 per month all-in (fees plus ad spend) is a realistic floor for paid campaigns that generate useful data. Below that, focus on one channel — usually Google Search or a well-optimised Google Business Profile — rather than spreading thinly.
