A/B Testing for South African Businesses: How to Optimise Your Marketing

By Brandon Howard2026-10-127 min read

A/B testing — also called split testing — is the practice of showing two versions of something (an ad, a landing page, an email subject line) to different audiences simultaneously and measuring which performs better. For South African businesses serious about improving their digital marketing performance, it's one of the highest-leverage activities available.

What You Can A/B Test

  • Google Ads headlines and descriptions: Which message gets more clicks?
  • Landing page headlines: Does "Get a Free Quote" outperform "Request a Callback"?
  • CTA button text and colour: "Submit" vs "Get My Free Audit" — one consistently outperforms
  • Form length: 3 fields vs 6 fields — shorter usually wins for top-of-funnel
  • Email subject lines: Does personalisation improve open rates?
  • Social media ad creative: Image A vs Image B, video vs static
  • Pricing page layout: Which plan do visitors click first?

How to Run a Valid A/B Test

  1. Start with a hypothesis: "Changing the CTA from 'Submit' to 'Get My Free Quote' will increase form submissions because it communicates value"
  2. Test one variable at a time: If you change the headline AND the image simultaneously, you won't know which change caused the result
  3. Run the test long enough: Minimum 2 weeks and at least 100 conversions per variant before calling a winner
  4. Use statistical significance: A 55% vs 45% split with only 40 total conversions is not conclusive — you need enough data to trust the result
  5. Implement the winner and test again: Optimisation is continuous, not one-off

The Fastest Wins for SA Businesses

If you're new to A/B testing, start where the volume is highest and the change is easiest to implement. Google Ads headline testing is the quickest — you can set up a responsive search ad with multiple headline variations and Google will automatically show the best-performing combinations. Within 4 weeks of sufficient traffic, you'll have clear data on which messages resonate.

South African-specific insight: Test whether adding "South Africa" or your city name to headlines improves click-through rates. In many categories, location-specific headlines outperform generic ones in SA search results because they signal relevance to local searchers.

How Much Traffic Do You Need?

A/B tests need enough conversions to tell real differences from chance. As a rough rule, aim for at least 100 conversions per version before drawing conclusions, and more for small differences. A page with 1,000 visitors and 20 enquiries a month can't run reliable tests on small tweaks — but it can test bold changes, like a completely different offer or page layout. Use an online sample size calculator before you start.

Testing Without Dedicated Software

  • Google Ads experiments let you split traffic between campaign versions or landing pages.
  • Ad variations — Google and Meta automatically rotate and compare ad versions.
  • Email A/B tests are built into most email platforms (subject lines, send times, content).
  • Sequential testing — change one thing and compare equal periods before and after, controlling for seasonality.

Common A/B Testing Mistakes

  • Stopping a test as soon as one version looks ahead.
  • Testing several changes at once, so you don't know what made the difference.
  • Running tests over unusual periods such as month-end, payday or public holidays without accounting for them.
  • Measuring clicks instead of the outcome you care about — enquiries or sales.
  • Not writing down results, so the same ideas get tested again.

What to Test First

Test the things most likely to change behaviour: the offer, the headline, the form length, and the call to action. Button colours rarely matter. See conversion rate optimisation and landing page optimisation, or get a free audit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should an A/B test run?

At least one to two full weeks to cover weekly patterns, and until each version has enough conversions — often 100 or more — for a reliable result.

What replaced Google Optimize?

Google Optimize closed in 2023. Alternatives include VWO, Optimizely, AB Tasty and Convert, plus built-in experiment tools in Google Ads, Meta and email platforms.

Can small websites do A/B testing?

Small sites can't reliably test minor tweaks, but they can test bold changes such as different offers or layouts, or compare equal periods before and after a single change.
