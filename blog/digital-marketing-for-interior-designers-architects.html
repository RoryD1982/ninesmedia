Digital Marketing for Interior Designers and Architects

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Your portfolio is your most powerful marketing asset — present projects as stories.
  • Instagram and Pinterest drive discovery; your website and SEO drive enquiries.
  • Qualify leads with budget and scope questions to protect your time.

Interior designers and architects sell a vision. Clients need to see your work, understand your process and feel confident you'll deliver within their budget. Digital marketing should showcase work beautifully and bring in well-qualified enquiries.

A Portfolio Website That Converts

  • Projects presented as stories: the brief, the challenge, your approach, the result.
  • High-quality photography (professional photos are worth the investment).
  • Clear service pages: residential, commercial, renovation, new builds.
  • Your process, from first meeting to completion.
  • Guidance on fees and typical project ranges.
  • Professional registration where relevant (for example SACAP for architects).

Instagram and Pinterest

Visual platforms drive discovery. Share finished spaces, before-and-afters, details, materials and behind-the-scenes site visits. Pinterest works well for interiors because people save ideas for future projects. Link back to project pages on your website.

SEO for Project Searches

People search for specific services and areas: "interior designer Sandton", "kitchen design Cape Town", "home extension architect Pretoria". Create project and service pages that match these searches, with locations mentioned naturally. See SEO in Johannesburg.

Google Ads

Ads can work for high-value services (residential architecture, commercial fit-outs, full interior design), but qualify carefully: many searchers want free ideas or very small jobs. Use specific keywords and clear fee guidance on landing pages.

Referrals and Partnerships

Builders, property developers, estate agents and suppliers refer clients. Keep relationships active, share your work with them and make referring you easy.

Qualify Enquiries

Ask about project type, location, timeline and budget range in your enquiry form. It saves both sides time and lets you prioritise the right projects.

See also property developers and construction marketing.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • A portfolio of beautiful photos with no project context or outcome.
  • No indication of fees or minimum project size, leading to many unsuitable enquiries.
  • Posting only finished projects — process content builds trust and engagement.
  • Not crediting photographers, suppliers and collaborators, who often share and refer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do interior designers get clients online?

A strong portfolio website, Instagram and Pinterest for discovery, SEO for local project searches, and referrals from builders and agents.

Should architects use Google Ads?

For high-value services, yes, with specific keywords and fee guidance on landing pages to filter out very small or free-idea enquiries.

How can designers avoid poor-fit enquiries?

Ask about project type, timeline and budget range on the enquiry form, and publish guidance on fees.
