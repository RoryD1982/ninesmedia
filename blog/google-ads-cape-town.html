Google Ads Cape Town: What Local Businesses Need to Know in 2026

By Brandon Howard 2026-10-08 6 min read

Cape Town is one of the most competitive digital advertising markets in South Africa. A thriving tourism industry, a dense concentration of SMEs in the Atlantic Seaboard and CBD, and a large, digitally-savvy consumer base make Google Ads simultaneously more competitive and more rewarding than in many other SA cities.

If you're running — or considering — Google Ads in Cape Town, here's what you need to know.

Cape Town Keyword Strategy: Suburbs Matter

In Cape Town, suburb-level targeting is critical. "Plumber Cape Town" has very different competition than "plumber Camps Bay" or "plumber Bellville". Include suburb-specific keywords in your campaigns — many searchers use suburbs by habit, and competition for those terms is often far lower than the city-level keyword.

High-value Cape Town suburb keywords to consider:

  • Atlantic Seaboard, Green Point, Sea Point (affluent, high intent)
  • Claremont, Rondebosch, Newlands (Southern Suburbs professional hub)
  • Bellville, Tygervalley (Northern Suburbs business centre)
  • Stellenbosch (adjacent market, separate campaign)

Seasonal Patterns in Cape Town

Cape Town's tourism economy creates unusual seasonal patterns in search volume. Accommodation, tourism, restaurant and hospitality businesses see peak search volume October–March (summer season). Service businesses (trades, medical, professional services) see more stable year-round volume with a slight boost in Q1 when businesses are planning for the year.

Budget accordingly — increase ad spend in your peak season and pull back in your off-peak months rather than maintaining a flat budget year-round.

What Makes Cape Town Google Ads Different

  • International audience: Cape Town's tourism market means some campaigns attract international searchers. If you're a tour operator or accommodation provider, consider separate campaigns targeting UK, Germany, and US searchers.
  • Bilingual market: Afrikaans-speaking consumers in the Northern Suburbs and Winelands search in Afrikaans. Add Afrikaans keyword variants for maximum coverage.
  • Mobile-first: Over 65% of local searches happen on mobile. If your landing page isn't optimised for mobile, you're wasting a significant portion of your budget.

Structuring a Cape Town Account

  • Area-based targeting — Atlantic Seaboard, City Bowl, Southern Suburbs, Northern Suburbs, Helderberg and the Winelands, with bids adjusted by results.
  • Separate campaigns for tourists and locals where relevant — visitors search from other provinces and abroad before arriving.
  • Service-specific landing pages that mention the areas you serve.
  • Call, WhatsApp and booking tracking so you can see which keywords produce customers.

Advertising to Visitors Before They Arrive

Tour operators, restaurants, activities and accommodation can reach travellers while they plan. Target people searching for Cape Town experiences from Gauteng, the UK, Europe and the US, and use keywords that include "Cape Town" plus the activity. Remember that "people in or interested in" location settings are useful here — unlike for local service businesses, where you should target people physically in your area.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Google Ads cost in Cape Town?

It depends on industry and competition. Many Cape Town businesses spend R3,000 to R15,000 a month on ads, with tourism and legal keywords among the more competitive.

Can I target tourists with Google Ads before they visit Cape Town?

Yes. You can target people in other provinces or countries who search for Cape Town activities and accommodation, using location settings that include people interested in Cape Town.

When is Google Ads most competitive in Cape Town?

Tourism and hospitality keywords are most competitive in the summer season, especially December to March.
