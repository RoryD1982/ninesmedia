How to Run Facebook Ads That Actually Convert in South Africa

Nines Media Team Updated 9 October 2026 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Choose the objective that matches the business result you want — Leads or Sales, not Engagement or Traffic.
  • Install the Meta Pixel and Conversions API so Meta can find people who convert.
  • Simplify structure: fewer campaigns and ad sets, broader audiences, more creative variety.
  • Creative is now the targeting — test 3–5 distinct concepts, with a hook in the first three seconds of video.

Facebook and Instagram ads can be one of the most cost-effective channels available to South African businesses. They can also be a quick way to spend money on likes that never become customers. The difference is rarely the budget. It's setup, structure and creative.

Mistake 1: The wrong objective

Meta optimises for exactly what you ask. Choose Engagement and it finds people who like things. Choose Traffic and it finds people who click but rarely buy. If you want leads or sales, use:

  • Leads — instant forms, Messenger, WhatsApp or calls.
  • Sales — purchases on your website (requires a working pixel).
  • Engagement → Messages — click-to-WhatsApp, if your team can reply quickly.

Avoid the "Boost post" button for anything tied to revenue.

Mistake 2: Broken tracking

Without accurate tracking, Meta can't learn who converts.

  • Install the Meta Pixel on every page.
  • Add the Conversions API to send events server-side; it recovers data lost to browser privacy settings.
  • Verify your domain and configure your priority events.
  • Pass purchase values for e-commerce.

Mistake 3: Over-complicated structure

Ten ad sets with R50 a day each will spend months in the learning phase. Simpler accounts perform better:

  • One prospecting campaign using Advantage+ or broad audiences.
  • One retargeting campaign for website visitors, video viewers and engagers (if volume justifies it).
  • Enough budget per ad set to reach roughly 50 conversions per week where possible.

For e-commerce, Advantage+ Shopping campaigns combine prospecting and retargeting in one.

Mistake 4: Creative that doesn't stop the scroll

With broad targeting, your creative effectively chooses your audience. What works in South Africa right now:

  • Vertical video (9:16), 15–30 seconds, with a hook in the first three seconds.
  • Captions and text overlays — most people watch without sound.
  • Real people and real results — customer testimonials, behind the scenes, before and after (within Meta's policies).
  • Local context — South African voices, places and references outperform generic stock.
  • Variety — three to five genuinely different concepts per campaign, not five colour variations of one image.

Mistake 5: Weak offers

"Contact us today" is not an offer. Try free consultations, limited-time pricing, bundles, guarantees, free delivery thresholds or a downloadable guide.

Mistake 6: Poor post-click experience

If your ad promises one thing and your landing page says another, conversion rates collapse. Keep the message consistent, make the page fast on mobile, and use WhatsApp buttons where possible.

Mistake 7: Judging results too quickly

Meta's algorithm needs time and data. Avoid editing campaigns daily. Judge new campaigns after at least seven days and a meaningful number of results.

A simple campaign framework

Stage Audience Creative Objective
Prospecting Broad / Advantage+ Hooks, problem-solution, social proof Leads or Sales
Retargeting Visitors, engagers, video viewers Testimonials, offers, FAQs Leads or Sales
Retention Customer lists New products, referrals Sales

Measure what matters

Track cost per lead, cost per qualified lead, cost per purchase, return on ad spend and — ideally — revenue in your CRM. Platform-reported results are directional; your sales data is the source of truth. For benchmarks, read how much Facebook ads cost in South Africa.

Frequently asked questions

Why are my Facebook ads not converting?

The most common reasons are the wrong campaign objective, broken or missing pixel tracking, audiences that are too narrow, weak creative, and landing pages or forms that don't match the ad. Fix tracking and objective first, then creative.

Should I use Advantage+ audiences or detailed targeting?

For most accounts with conversion history, Advantage+ or broad audiences now outperform heavily stacked interests, because Meta's algorithm finds buyers based on behaviour. Detailed targeting can still help with new accounts or very niche B2B offers.

Are Facebook lead forms or landing pages better?

Instant forms usually produce cheaper leads, landing pages usually produce better-quality ones. Use higher-intent forms with a review screen and qualifying questions, and compare cost per qualified lead, not just cost per lead.

How often should I change my Facebook ad creative?

Refresh creative every four to six weeks, or sooner if frequency rises above three and cost per result starts climbing.

Do click-to-WhatsApp ads work in South Africa?

Often very well, because many South Africans prefer messaging to forms or calls. They suit services, retail and local businesses that can respond quickly.
