How to Run Facebook Ads That Actually Convert in South Africa

By Brandon Howard Brandon — Nines Media 2026-06-25 8 min read

Most South African businesses that try Meta advertising and give up are right that their campaigns weren't working — but wrong about why. The problem is almost never Facebook or Instagram's reach. It's almost always one of five fixable issues: wrong objective, wrong audience, weak creative, no tracking, or no follow-up system. Here's how to diagnose and fix each one.

1. Wrong Objective

Meta's campaign objective tells the algorithm what to optimise for. Choosing "Traffic" when you want leads means Meta will find people most likely to click — not most likely to fill in a form. Choosing "Brand Awareness" when you want sales means Meta will maximise reach — not conversions.

For lead generation: use Leads objective with an Instant Form (Meta's native lead form), or Conversions objective pointing to a landing page with a thank-you page for tracking. For e-commerce: use Sales objective with the Meta Pixel properly configured. Choosing the right objective is the most important decision you make in campaign setup.

2. Audience Too Narrow or Too Broad

South Africa's Facebook audience is relatively small by global standards — roughly 18 million active users. This means audience targeting requires care. Too narrow (under 50,000) and your CPMs spike and frequency becomes annoying. Too broad (over 5 million) and Meta struggles to find your ideal customer within budget.

The sweet spot for most SA service businesses: 200,000–1,500,000 people. Use interest and behaviour targeting to reach relevant people, or — better — build Custom Audiences from your website visitors, customer email list, or video viewers, and then create Lookalike Audiences that mirror your best existing customers.

3. Creative That Doesn't Stop the Scroll

In 2026, South African users scroll faster and are more ad-literate than ever. The first 1–2 seconds of your video — or the first frame of your image — determine whether anyone watches or reads further. Creative rules that consistently work in the SA market:

  • Show a real person or real customer result immediately — not a logo or a tagline
  • Lead with the problem, not the solution: "Still paying too much for your insurance?" beats "Affordable insurance for South Africans"
  • Use subtitles on all video — most SA users watch with sound off
  • Reels-format (vertical 9:16) outperforms square on mobile across all placements
  • Test UGC-style (casual, phone-shot) creative against polished studio work — the former often wins

4. No Conversion Tracking

If the Meta Pixel isn't installed and configured on your website, you're running ads blind. The Pixel tracks which ads lead to which actions (form fills, purchases, page visits), enabling Meta's algorithm to find more people like those who converted. Without it, your campaign cannot improve. Install the Pixel through Meta Business Manager and verify it's firing correctly using Meta's Pixel Helper Chrome extension.

For lead forms inside Meta (Instant Forms), connect them to a CRM via Zapier or Meta's native CRM integrations so leads are captured and actioned immediately — not sitting in Meta Ads Manager waiting to be downloaded.

5. No Follow-Up System

Meta leads are different from Google leads. Someone who searched on Google was actively looking for you. Someone who filled in a Meta form responded to an ad while they were scrolling — they may not remember your brand 20 minutes later. Speed and persistence of follow-up are critical. Call or WhatsApp within 5–10 minutes of the lead coming in. If no answer, follow up again at different times of day. A Meta lead that isn't followed up within an hour has dramatically lower conversion probability.
