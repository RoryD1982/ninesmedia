SEO Packages in South Africa: What Should Be Included?

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Compare SEO packages by deliverables and reporting, not by the number of keywords promised.
  • Every package should include technical fixes, on-page work, content and clear reporting.
  • Ask how the package changes after month three; SEO needs to adapt to what the data shows.

SEO packages in South Africa are hard to compare. One provider offers "20 keywords", another "4 blog posts", another "monthly optimisation". This guide sets out what a good package should actually contain, so you can compare like with like. For price ranges, see SEO pricing in South Africa.

What Every SEO Package Should Include

1. Technical SEO

  • An initial technical audit and fixes for indexing, crawl errors, speed and mobile issues.
  • Ongoing monitoring of Search Console for new problems.
  • Structured data (schema) where relevant.

2. On-page optimisation

  • Keyword mapping: which page targets which searches.
  • Improved titles, headings, content and internal links on key pages.
  • Fixing pages that compete with each other for the same search.

3. Content

  • New or expanded pages for services and questions your customers search for.
  • A content plan based on search data, not guesses.

4. Local SEO (for local businesses)

  • Google Business Profile optimisation and posts.
  • Review strategy and consistent business listings.

5. Authority

  • Earning genuine mentions and links from relevant South African sites — not bulk link packages.

6. Reporting

  • Organic traffic, impressions and rankings for searches that matter.
  • Enquiries and sales from organic search.
  • What was done this month and what's planned next.

Typical Package Levels

LevelFocusTypical monthly range
LocalGoogle Business Profile, key service pages, local citationsR3,000 – R6,000
GrowthTechnical, on-page, 2–4 pages of content, outreachR6,000 – R15,000
Competitive / nationalLarger content programme, digital PR, technical depthR15,000+

Package Red Flags

  • A fixed number of backlinks every month.
  • "Guaranteed page one" promises.
  • Identical packages for every business regardless of competition.
  • Reports with rankings but no traffic or enquiry data.
  • No access to Search Console or analytics.

Questions to Ask

  1. What exactly will be delivered in months one to three?
  2. Which pages and searches will you focus on first, and why?
  3. How do you earn links, and can I see examples?
  4. How will you report on enquiries from organic search?

Frequently Asked Questions

What should an SEO package include?

Technical SEO, on-page optimisation, content, local SEO where relevant, genuine authority building and reporting on traffic and enquiries.

How much are SEO packages in South Africa?

Local packages typically start around R3,000 to R6,000 a month, growth packages R6,000 to R15,000, and competitive national packages R15,000 or more.

Are cheap SEO packages worth it?

Be careful. Very cheap packages often rely on automated reports and bulk links. A smaller, honest scope is better value.
