Google Ads for Doctors and Medical Practices in South Africa

By Brandon Howard2026-10-137 min read

Google Ads is one of the fastest ways for South African medical practices to attract new patients. When someone types "GP near me" or "dermatologist Cape Town" into Google, they have immediate, high intent. Appearing at the top of those results can fill appointment slots and reduce dependence on referral networks.

Why Google Ads Works for Medical Practices

Unlike brand awareness advertising, Google Search Ads capture patients at the exact moment they're looking for care. You're not interrupting someone who isn't interested — you're appearing for someone who has already decided they need a doctor and is now choosing which one to call. That intent alignment is why healthcare is one of the strongest-performing verticals for Google Ads.

Campaign Structure for Medical Practices

Structure your campaigns by specialty or service, not by location. Each service should have its own campaign with dedicated ad groups and landing pages:

  • General practice / GP services
  • Specific specialties: dermatology, cardiology, sports medicine, etc.
  • Specific procedures: mole removal, vasectomy, minor surgery
  • Preventive health: annual checkups, health screening packages

This structure lets you control budget per service, measure which services are attracting patients, and write highly relevant ad copy for each.

Keyword Strategy

Focus on transactional and local keywords — people who are ready to book, not just researching:

  • "GP [suburb] open Saturday"
  • "Dermatologist Johannesburg appointment"
  • "Sports medicine doctor Cape Town"
  • "Same-day doctor appointment [city]"

Use phrase and exact match types. Negative keywords are critical in healthcare — exclude "salary," "jobs," "course," "training" and condition-research terms if you only want appointment-ready patients.

HPCSA Advertising Guidelines

South African healthcare practitioners advertising on Google must comply with HPCSA regulations. Key rules:

  • You may not make comparative claims (claiming to be "the best" or comparing yourself to competitors)
  • You may not advertise fees unless in certain approved formats
  • You may not use testimonials or endorsements in a way that implies guaranteed outcomes
  • Advertising must be factual, dignified and not create unrealistic expectations
  • Your HPCSA registration number should appear on your website (not required in ads but expected on the destination page)

Google's own healthcare advertising policies also restrict certain medical categories. Before launching, verify your practice category is eligible and that your landing pages comply with both HPCSA and Google policies.

Important: Some medical specialties (fertility treatments, certain cosmetic procedures) face additional restrictions on Google Ads. Work with an agency that understands healthcare advertising compliance — a disapproved account can take weeks to reinstate.

Landing Pages for Medical Practices

Don't send ad traffic to your homepage. Build dedicated landing pages for each service or campaign with:

  • A clear headline matching the ad's promise ("Book a Same-Day GP Appointment in Rondebosch")
  • Your location, hours and parking information
  • Medical aid schemes you're registered with
  • The doctor's credentials and photo (patients want to know who they're seeing)
  • A simple booking form or click-to-call button as the primary CTA

Measuring Success

Track phone calls, online bookings and form submissions as conversions in Google Ads. This data drives smart bidding algorithms — Target CPA bidding tells Google to optimise for patient enquiries, not just clicks. Once you have 30+ conversions, smart bidding typically outperforms manual bid management.
