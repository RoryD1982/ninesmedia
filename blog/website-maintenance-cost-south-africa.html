Website Maintenance Costs in South Africa: What You Should Pay Monthly

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Most small business websites cost a few hundred rand a month to run, plus optional maintenance.
  • Typical maintenance plans range from about R500 to R2,000+ a month depending on what's included.
  • WordPress sites need regular updates and backups; skipping them is a security risk.

The cost of a website doesn't end at launch. Hosting, domains, security updates and changes all cost something each month. Here's a breakdown of realistic monthly costs for South African businesses.

Typical Monthly Running Costs

ItemTypical costNotes
Domain (.co.za)Roughly R100–R300 a yearRenew annually; keep it in your name
Web hostingR100–R500/month for most SMEsShared or managed WordPress hosting
Email hostingOften per user per monthGoogle Workspace or Microsoft 365
Maintenance planR500–R2,000+/monthUpdates, backups, security, small changes
Premium plugins / appsVariesForms, SEO, booking or e-commerce add-ons

These are general market ranges; check your provider's current pricing.

What a Maintenance Plan Should Include

  • Core, theme and plugin updates (for WordPress).
  • Regular off-site backups, with tested restores.
  • Security monitoring and malware scanning.
  • Uptime monitoring.
  • SSL certificate renewal.
  • A set amount of time for small content changes.
  • A monthly report of what was done.

Website Builders vs Self-Hosted Sites

Builders like Wix, Squarespace and Shopify bundle hosting, security and updates into a monthly subscription — simpler, but less flexible. Self-hosted WordPress sites usually cost less for hosting but need regular maintenance. Custom static sites (like ours) need very little maintenance but require a developer for changes. See WordPress vs Shopify.

What You Can Skip

  • Premium plugins you don't use.
  • Expensive hosting for a small brochure site with low traffic.
  • Maintenance plans with lots of "hours" you never use — pay for what you need.

What You Shouldn't Skip

Updates and backups. Outdated WordPress plugins are a common way sites get hacked, and recovering a hacked site without a backup costs far more than a year of maintenance. See protecting your site.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost per month in South Africa?

Most small business websites cost roughly R100 to R500 a month for hosting plus a small annual domain fee. Optional maintenance plans typically add R500 to R2,000 or more.

Do I need a website maintenance plan?

For WordPress sites, regular updates and backups are essential. You can do them yourself or pay for a plan; skipping them is a security risk.

Is website hosting included in the website price?

Usually not. Build costs and ongoing hosting, domain and maintenance costs are normally separate.
