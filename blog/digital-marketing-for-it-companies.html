Industry Guides

Digital Marketing for IT and Managed Service Providers

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Clear positioning by industry or service beats generic 'IT solutions' messaging.
  • Specific searches like 'IT support for law firms Johannesburg' convert well.
  • Proof — case studies, certifications and response times — wins contracts.

IT companies and managed service providers (MSPs) in South Africa often sound the same: "end-to-end IT solutions". Buyers can't tell them apart, so they choose on price or referral. Digital marketing works best when you're specific about who you help and how.

Position Clearly

Choose a focus: an industry (legal, healthcare, logistics), a service (cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, cloud migration) or a business size. Specific positioning makes your marketing more relevant and your content more credible.

Service Pages That Rank

  • One page per service: managed IT support, cybersecurity, backup and recovery, cloud, networking.
  • Industry pages if you specialise.
  • Clear service levels: response times, support hours, on-site areas.
  • Partner certifications (Microsoft, cybersecurity vendors) shown clearly.
  • Case studies with measurable outcomes.

Google Ads for IT Services

Target high-intent searches like "IT support company Sandton", "managed IT services Pretoria" or "ransomware recovery help". Avoid broad terms that attract job seekers and students. Urgent searches (outages, breaches) suit call-focused ads. See call ads.

LinkedIn and Content

IT buyers — business owners, operations managers, CFOs — value practical advice. Share content on POPIA compliance for IT systems, cybersecurity checklists, load management and remote work setups. Founders posting regularly on LinkedIn often generate more enquiries than company pages. See LinkedIn content strategy.

Partnerships and Referrals

Accountants, auditors, business consultants and software vendors refer IT providers. A clear partner page and simple referral process help.

Nurture and Measure

IT contracts take time. Use email nurturing and remarketing, follow up quotes systematically, and track enquiries through to signed contracts. See B2B digital marketing.

Mistakes IT Companies Make Online

  • Leading with technology jargon instead of business outcomes (uptime, security, productivity).
  • Listing every vendor logo but no case studies.
  • Bidding on broad terms like "IT" that attract job seekers and students.
  • No clear pricing model — per user, per device or fixed — so buyers can't gauge cost.
  • Slow responses to enquiries from a business that sells responsiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can an IT company get more clients?

Position clearly by industry or service, build strong service pages with proof, run targeted search ads for high-intent IT searches, and use LinkedIn content and referral partnerships.

Do Google Ads work for IT support companies?

Yes, for specific high-intent searches in the areas you serve, with negative keywords to filter out job and training searches.

What content should an MSP publish?

Practical guidance on cybersecurity, compliance, cloud and backup, plus case studies showing measurable outcomes.
