Google Ads Specialist South Africa: What to Look for and What to Pay

By Brandon Howard2026-10-137 min read

Poor Google Ads management is one of the biggest drains on South African business marketing budgets. With the wrong person running your campaigns, you can easily burn R20,000 a month with nothing to show for it. With the right specialist, that same budget can generate consistent, scalable revenue.

What a Google Ads Specialist Does

A skilled Google Ads specialist is part data analyst, part copywriter, part conversion strategist. Their core responsibilities include:

  • Account architecture — structuring campaigns and ad groups for maximum Quality Score and relevance
  • Keyword strategy — identifying high-intent terms, building negative keyword lists to prevent wasted spend, and balancing broad, phrase and exact match types
  • Ad copy and creative — writing compelling responsive search ads, headlines and descriptions that earn clicks from the right people
  • Bid management — using smart bidding (Target CPA, Target ROAS) or manual bidding strategies appropriate to the account's maturity
  • Conversion tracking — setting up and verifying that leads, calls, purchases and micro-conversions are accurately measured
  • Landing page feedback — identifying where landing pages are leaking conversions and recommending improvements
  • Reporting — translating performance data into actionable insights, not just vanity metrics

Some agencies charge a percentage of ad spend (typically 10–20%). This creates a perverse incentive to increase spend regardless of efficiency. Flat management fees or performance-linked retainers better align the agency's incentives with your outcomes.

The Difference Between Good and Great Google Ads Management

Good: The account is set up correctly, ads are running, some optimisation happens monthly, basic reports are sent.

Great: The specialist understands your sales cycle, knows which campaigns feed top-of-funnel vs bottom-of-funnel, continuously tests new ad variations, is proactive about opportunities (new match types, new campaign formats), and connects ad performance to actual business outcomes — revenue, not just clicks.

Google Certifications and Partner Status

Ask whether the specialist or agency is a Google Partner. Google Partner agencies must have certified employees and meet performance standards. It's a baseline credential, not a guarantee of excellence, but it does filter out the most inexperienced operators.

One test: Ask any Google Ads candidate to explain their approach to negative keywords. A strong answer covers how they build initial lists, how they mine search term reports weekly, and how they balance blocking irrelevant traffic without over-filtering. A weak answer is vague or treats negatives as a one-time setup task.

When to Consider an In-House Hire vs an Agency

If your Google Ads spend exceeds R80,000–R100,000/month, the economics of a dedicated in-house specialist may make sense. Below that threshold, an agency typically delivers better value: you get a team rather than an individual, there's cover when your account manager is sick or leaves, and the agency's cross-client experience means insights from other industries flow into your account.
