Most businesses don't need more marketing activity — they need the right activity, measured properly. As a digital marketing agency, our job is to work out which channels will bring you customers at a cost that makes sense, run them well, and show you exactly what you're getting for your money.

Our Digital Marketing Services

Google Ads & PPC

Search, Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube campaigns managed by a certified Google Partner team.

Meta, Facebook & Instagram ads

Lead generation, sales and retargeting campaigns with creative built for each format.

Social media management

Content calendars, design, copy, community management and reporting across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok.

SEO

Branding & web design

You can take one service or combine several. Working with a smaller budget? See our affordable digital marketing packages for realistic all-in costs. Most clients start with the channel closest to revenue — usually paid search — and add SEO, social and content as results come in.

How We Work

  1. Free audit. A senior strategist reviews your website, ads, social presence and competitors, and sends a written report covering your biggest growth levers and where budget is being wasted.
  2. Strategy before tactics. We agree targets — leads, sales, cost per acquisition — and the channel mix to hit them. (Here's why strategy has to come first.)
  3. Build and launch. Tracking, campaigns, content and landing pages are set up properly before money is spent.
  4. Optimise and report. Ongoing optimisation (weekly on most packages), with reporting in leads and revenue rather than likes and impressions.

Whoever you hire, ask to see how they'll measure success, confirm you'll own your accounts, and avoid long lock-in contracts or guaranteed rankings. We've written a full checklist in how to choose a digital marketing agency, and if you're weighing up options, agency vs in-house vs freelancer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency plans and runs your online marketing: paid advertising on Google and Meta, search engine optimisation, social media, content, email and websites. A good agency ties all of it to commercial targets like leads and sales, and reports on them.

How much does a digital marketing agency cost in South Africa?

Monthly retainers in South Africa typically range from about R5,000 for a single channel to R40,000 or more for full-service work. Nines Media packages start from R2,500 (Google Ads Essentials), R5,000 (Starter), R12,000 (Growth) and R25,000 (Full Service) a month, with ad spend billed separately.

Where is Nines Media based?

Our office is at 1st Floor, Dainfern Square, Cnr William Nicol & Broadacres Drive, Johannesburg. We work with businesses throughout South Africa.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Google Ads Essentials from R2,500 a month suits small ad budgets, and our Starter package from R5,000 a month is designed for SMEs taking their first serious step into digital marketing, focusing budget on the single channel most likely to produce customers.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. All packages are month-to-month, and you keep ownership of your ad accounts, analytics and website.

How do I get started?

Request a free audit. A senior strategist reviews your current digital presence and sends a written report with recommendations and a tailored quote — there's no obligation to sign.
