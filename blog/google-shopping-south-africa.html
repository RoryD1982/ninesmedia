Google Shopping Ads South Africa: A Guide for Online Retailers

By Brandon Howard2026-10-127 min read

Google Shopping ads — the product listings that appear at the top of Google with images, prices, and store names — are one of the highest-converting ad formats available for South African online retailers. Here's how to set them up and make them work.

How Google Shopping Works

Unlike text-based Google Ads where you choose keywords to bid on, Shopping ads are driven by your product data feed. You submit a feed of all your products (with titles, descriptions, prices, and images) to Google Merchant Centre, and Google automatically matches your products to relevant searches. The ads appear at the top of search results for product-intent queries.

Setting Up Google Shopping in South Africa

  1. Create a Google Merchant Centre account at merchants.google.com — verify and claim your website
  2. Set up your product feed — this can be done manually (for small catalogues), via a Shopify/WooCommerce plugin, or via a Google Sheets template
  3. Ensure your feed meets Google's requirements: accurate pricing, in-stock status, clear titles, and compliant images (no watermarks, white backgrounds for most categories)
  4. Link Merchant Centre to your Google Ads account
  5. Create a Shopping campaign in Google Ads targeting South Africa

Product Feed Optimisation

Your product feed is the engine of your Shopping campaigns. Poor feed quality = poor performance. Key optimisation areas:

  • Product titles: Include brand, product type, key attributes (colour, size, material) — "Nike Air Max 90 White Running Shoes Mens Size 10 SA" outperforms "Running Shoe"
  • Product descriptions: Use relevant search terms naturally; describe what makes the product unique
  • GTIN numbers: Include Global Trade Item Numbers (barcodes) for branded products — improves eligibility and performance
  • Images: High-quality, white background for core image; lifestyle images in additional image slots

Performance Max vs Standard Shopping

Google has largely moved to Performance Max (PMax) campaigns which incorporate Shopping inventory alongside Search, Display, and YouTube. PMax uses AI to optimise across all channels but requires more budget and time to learn. Standard Shopping campaigns give more control and transparency — recommended for most SA e-commerce businesses starting out with a modest budget.

SA-specific note: South Africa uses South African Rand (ZAR) as its currency. Ensure your Merchant Centre is set to ZAR and your pricing is displayed correctly. Google requires pricing in your Merchant Centre to match your website exactly — any discrepancy leads to product disapprovals.

Free Listings First

Once your products are in Google Merchant Center, they can appear in free listings on Google's Shopping tab and other surfaces without ad spend. Set this up even before running paid Shopping campaigns — it's free traffic and a good test that your feed is approved.

Common Merchant Center Problems

  • Price or availability mismatch between the feed and website — keep the feed synced automatically.
  • Missing shipping and returns information — set delivery costs and times for South Africa in Merchant Center.
  • Missing identifiers such as GTINs (barcodes) for branded products.
  • Policy issues — restricted products, unclear contact details or missing refund policy on your website.

Structure for Profit

Don't treat every product the same. Separate products by margin and performance (for example using custom labels for high-margin, best-sellers and clearance), give profitable products more budget, and set realistic return on ad spend targets for each group. Exclude products that never sell profitably through ads.

Optimise Product Titles

Titles have the biggest influence on which searches your products appear for. Include brand, product type and key attributes — size, colour, material, model — in the order shoppers search. "Nike Air Max 90 Men's Running Shoe White Size 9" beats "Air Max 90".

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Shopping available in South Africa?

Yes. South African stores can list products in Google Merchant Center for free listings and run paid Shopping or Performance Max campaigns.

How much should I spend on Google Shopping?

Start with a budget that allows enough clicks to learn which products convert, often from R5,000 a month for small stores, then scale the products that are profitable.

Should I use Performance Max or Standard Shopping?

Performance Max often scales well with good feeds and conversion data. Standard Shopping gives more control and transparency, which helps with tight budgets or testing.
