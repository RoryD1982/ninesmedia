How to Set Your Digital Marketing Budget in South Africa

By Nines Media2026-10-128 min read

One of the most common questions South African business owners ask is: "How much should I spend on digital marketing?" The honest answer depends on your revenue, growth goals, competitive landscape, and which channels you're investing in. This guide gives you a practical framework to arrive at the right number for your business.

The Revenue Percentage Rule

The most widely used benchmark for digital marketing spend is 5–12% of revenue:

  • Maintenance (5–7%): If your goal is to hold your current market position in a stable market
  • Growth (8–12%): If you're in a competitive market or actively trying to grow market share
  • Aggressive growth (15%+): If you're in a fast-growing sector or launching a new product/service

These percentages apply to total marketing spend — digital plus traditional. For businesses that have shifted entirely to digital, the same percentages apply to digital spend alone.

How to Allocate Your Budget Across Channels

A common starting allocation for SA service businesses:

  • Google Ads (ad spend): 40–50% of total budget
  • Agency management fees (Google + Meta): 20–25%
  • Meta Ads (ad spend): 15–20%
  • SEO: 15–20%
  • Content and creative: 10–15%

E-commerce businesses typically allocate more to Google Shopping and Meta retargeting; B2B businesses allocate more to LinkedIn and SEO.

Start Lean, Scale What Works

The worst approach is spreading a limited budget thinly across many channels. With R10,000/month, you're better served running one well-funded Google Ads campaign than running three under-resourced campaigns across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn. Once you have data on what drives leads and at what cost, you can expand into additional channels with confidence.

Agency fees vs ad spend: Always separate these in your budget planning. A R15,000/month "digital marketing package" that includes both agency fees and ad spend is opaque — insist on knowing exactly how much goes to the agency and how much is spent on ads placing your message in front of customers.
