Social Media Management Packages & Pricing in South Africa (2026)

Nines Media Team Updated 9 October 2026 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Social media management in South Africa costs roughly R2,000–R50,000+ per month, depending on platforms, content volume and video.
  • R8,000–R20,000 is the level where most established SMEs get original content, community management and useful reporting.
  • Paid social (ad spend) is almost always charged on top of the management fee.
  • Pick one or two platforms where your customers actually are; quality beats posting everywhere.

Ask five South African agencies for a social media quote and you will get five very different numbers — anything from R2,000 to R50,000 a month. The gap is not random. It comes down to what is actually being delivered: scheduled template posts, or a content engine that builds an audience and drives sales.

This guide explains what social media packages include, the price tiers in the South African market, and how to make sure you are paying for results rather than activity.

What is included in social media management?

Social media management is a bundle of separate jobs. The more of them a package covers — and the more original the work — the higher the price.

  • Strategy and planning — audience research, content pillars, monthly calendars, campaign themes.
  • Content creation — copywriting, graphic design, photography and short-form video. This is the most time-intensive part and the biggest driver of cost.
  • Publishing — scheduling across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok.
  • Community management — replying to comments, DMs and reviews, often daily.
  • Paid social — Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ad campaigns, usually priced separately.
  • Reporting — performance against goals, not just follower counts.

Social media pricing tiers in South Africa (2026)

These are monthly management fees and exclude any ad budget paid to the platforms.

Tier Monthly fee Posts per month Platforms What to expect
Entry R2,000–R8,000 8–12 1–2 Scheduled posts, templated graphics, limited or no community management
Mid-tier R8,000–R20,000 16–24 2–3 Content strategy, original design, some short-form video, community management, monthly insights
Full service R20,000–R50,000 24+ 3–4 Dedicated creative team, regular video, paid social management, influencer coordination
Enterprise R50,000+ High volume Multiple brands Daily video, always-on community team, multi-brand reporting

Entry level: R2,000–R8,000 per month

This keeps your profiles active. It typically means a freelancer or junior team member posting templated content on one or two platforms. It suits very small businesses that need consistency but are not relying on social to drive revenue. Don't expect growth from it.

Mid-tier: R8,000–R20,000 per month

For most established South African SMEs, this is the minimum level at which social media starts producing commercial results. You should get a content strategy tied to your business goals, original graphics and short videos, active replies to comments and messages, and monthly reporting that connects social activity to enquiries or sales.

Full service: R20,000–R50,000+ per month

This level suits brands where social is a primary acquisition channel — e-commerce, hospitality, retail and consumer brands. Expect frequent video, integrated paid campaigns, influencer partnerships and strategy reviews tied to revenue.

What about social media advertising costs?

Organic reach on Facebook and Instagram is low for most business pages, so most growth plans combine content with paid promotion. The good news is that South Africa is an inexpensive market to advertise in: third-party benchmarks consistently put South African Meta CPMs at a fraction of the global average. A realistic starting ad budget for a local business is R3,000–R10,000 per month, on top of management. Our guide to Facebook ads costs in South Africa goes into detail.

Which platforms should your package cover?

South Africa has more than 50 million internet users, but platform usage varies sharply by age and audience. According to DataReportal's Digital 2026: South Africa report, Facebook's ad audience was about 27.9 million, TikTok's adult ad audience about 29.1 million, LinkedIn had 17 million registered members and Instagram's ad audience was about 8.6 million.

  • Facebook — still the broadest reach for over-30s, community groups and local services.
  • Instagram — visual brands, beauty, fashion, food and hospitality.
  • TikTok — huge reach with under-35s and increasingly with older audiences; needs native video (TikTok ads guide).
  • LinkedIn — B2B, professional services and recruitment (LinkedIn guide).

Paying for five platforms rarely makes sense. Two platforms done well beat five done poorly.

How to tell if a social media package is good value

  1. Ask how many hours go into your account each month. Content production and community management take time; R3,000 cannot buy much of either.
  2. Check who creates the content. Original design and video should be in-house or clearly sourced, not stock templates reused across clients.
  3. Agree on the goal. Leads, sales, foot traffic or awareness — each needs different content and metrics.
  4. Look for business metrics in reports. Enquiries, clicks to WhatsApp, sales and cost per result, not just likes.
  5. Confirm you own everything. Your pages, ad accounts and creative files should stay with you.

Frequently asked questions

How much does social media management cost in South Africa?

Entry-level packages cost about R2,000–R8,000 per month, mid-tier packages R8,000–R20,000, and full-service management R20,000–R50,000 or more. The price depends on the number of platforms, posts per month, video production and whether community management and paid social are included.

How many posts should a social media package include?

Typical South African packages include 8–12 posts a month at entry level and 16–24 across two or three platforms at mid-tier. Consistency and quality matter more than volume — three strong posts a week usually beat daily filler.

Does a social media package include Facebook and Instagram ads?

Usually not. Most agencies charge paid social management separately or include it only in higher tiers, and the ad budget itself is always paid to Meta on top. Ask for the management fee and ad spend to be listed separately.

Is a freelancer or an agency better for social media?

A good freelancer can be excellent value for one or two platforms with simple content. An agency makes more sense when you need video, design, copywriting, paid social and reporting together, or when continuity matters if one person is unavailable.
