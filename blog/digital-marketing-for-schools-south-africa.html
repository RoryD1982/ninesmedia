Digital Marketing for Private Schools in South Africa

By Brandon Howard2026-10-126 min read

Private and independent schools in South Africa compete for a limited pool of families willing and able to pay independent school fees. Digital marketing — done well — gives schools a measurable way to reach those families at the exact moment they're evaluating options.

The Parent Search Journey

Most parents researching private schools begin on Google with searches like "best private school Sandton", "IEB school Cape Town", or "independent school near me". They then review the school's website, look at photos and videos, read Google reviews, and often visit the school's Facebook or Instagram page before making contact. Your digital presence needs to perform well at every stage of this journey.

Google Ads for School Enrolments

Target search terms that align with how parents search:

  • "private school [suburb/city]"
  • "IEB school near me"
  • "independent school [year level]"
  • "school open day [city]"

Landing pages for school ads should prominently feature fees (or a "request a fee schedule" CTA), accreditation, facilities, and a simple enquiry or tour booking form.

Social Media for Schools

Facebook remains dominant for reaching parents aged 30–50. Instagram is increasingly important for reaching aspirational families and for showcasing school culture. Posts showing real student life, sports days, academic achievements, and school culture convert better than polished marketing imagery alone.

Google Business Profile for Schools

A fully optimised GBP with accurate operating hours, photos, and a strong review profile (aim for 4.7+ stars from parents) is one of the highest-converting digital assets a school can have. Many parents make shortlists based on Google reviews alone.

Enrolment seasonality: School enquiry volume peaks in August–October for the following year. Run your highest-intensity digital marketing campaigns in this window. Year-round brand presence (social media, SEO) supports the conversion when that peak arrives.

Plan Around the Admissions Calendar

School marketing is seasonal. Most families apply a year or more before their child starts, and many independent schools fill Grade 0/R, Grade 1 and Grade 8 places well in advance. Build your campaigns around:

  • Open days and tours — run Google and Meta ads two to four weeks before each open day, with a simple booking form.
  • Application deadlines — remind interested families (and anyone who downloaded a prospectus) as deadlines approach.
  • Mid-year moves — families relocating for work search for schools at any time of year, so keep always-on search ads for "[school type] + [area]".

What Your School Website Must Answer

Parents compare several schools at once. The schools that win enquiries make the key information easy to find:

  • Curriculum (CAPS, IEB, Cambridge or IB) and grades offered.
  • Fees, or a clear way to request the fee schedule.
  • Class sizes, subject choices, sport and cultural activities.
  • Transport, aftercare and boarding options.
  • Results presented honestly, such as matric pass rates and university entrance passes.
  • A single, prominent way to book a tour or start an application.

Following Up With Enquiries

An enquiry is the start of a long decision. Schools that respond the same day, invite families to visit and send a short sequence of helpful emails (what a typical day looks like, how admissions work, meet the principal) convert far more enquiries into applications. Marketing automation can handle this without adding admin work.

Privacy and Children's Images

Collect parents' details with clear consent under POPIA, and only market to families who opted in. Be especially careful with photos of learners: get written permission from parents before using images in ads or on social media, and avoid naming children alongside their photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should a private school run enrolment ads?

Run focused campaigns before each open day and application deadline, and keep always-on search ads for families searching by area and curriculum, since relocating families look for schools all year.

Which social media platform is best for schools?

Facebook reaches the widest group of parents, while Instagram is useful for showing school life. Both work best when they show real learners and events, with parental consent.

Can schools use photos of learners in ads?

Only with written consent from parents. Under POPIA, schools should also collect parents' details with clear consent and avoid naming children alongside their images.
