Keyword Research for South African Businesses: A Practical Guide

By Brandon Howard2026-10-128 min read

Keyword research is the foundation of both SEO and Google Ads in South Africa. Get it right and every subsequent piece of content and every rand of ad spend is targeted at searches that matter. Get it wrong and you're ranking for terms no one searches, or bidding on clicks that never convert. Here's how to do it correctly for the SA market.

Understanding the South African Search Landscape

South African search behaviour has some distinct characteristics:

  • City-specific searches are very common: "digital marketing agency Johannesburg" vs just "digital marketing agency"
  • Price-intent keywords are high-volume: "how much does X cost in South Africa" performs well
  • Both "South Africa" and "SA" variants are used — target both
  • Township and suburb-level searches are growing with mobile penetration
  • Afrikaans keywords represent a significant opportunity in certain markets (Cape, Northern Cape, Highveld)

Free Keyword Research Tools

Google Keyword Planner

Free with a Google Ads account. Shows monthly search volume, competition level, and suggested bid for any keyword. Set the location to South Africa and language to English (or Afrikaans where relevant). The best starting point for any SA keyword research project.

Google Search Console

Shows what search queries are already bringing traffic to your site. Invaluable for finding keywords you rank for but haven't optimised, and identifying high-impression/low-click terms where improving your title tag could increase traffic.

Google Autocomplete and Related Searches

Type your primary keyword into Google and note the autocomplete suggestions and the "Related searches" at the bottom of the results page. These are real searches people make — and they're free data.

Keyword Classification for SA Businesses

Organise your target keywords into three intent categories:

  • Informational: "how to do X" — target with blog content
  • Commercial: "best X in [city]", "X cost South Africa" — target with service pages and Google Ads
  • Transactional: "hire X [city]", "book X near me" — target with Google Ads and optimised landing pages
SA-specific tip: Add "South Africa", "SA", and your primary city to every keyword variant and check the volume separately. "Google Ads agency" vs "Google Ads agency South Africa" can have very different volumes and CPC levels in the SA market.

Use South African Language

South Africans don't always search the way international tools assume. Check local terms and spellings:

  • "Geyser" not "water heater", "bakkie" not "pickup truck", "estate agent" alongside "realtor".
  • Local place names, suburbs and abbreviations (Joburg, JHB, PTA, CPT, KZN).
  • British spellings ("optimisation", "colour") alongside American ones.
  • Afrikaans terms for some local services and audiences.

Set tools to South Africa as the location; global volumes are misleading.

Prioritise by Value, Not Volume

A keyword with 50 monthly searches from people ready to buy is worth more than 5,000 from people researching. Score each keyword on:

  • Intent — is the searcher looking to buy, compare or learn?
  • Relevance — does it match a service you actually offer, in an area you serve?
  • Difficulty — who already ranks, and could you realistically compete?

Turn Keywords Into Pages and Ad Groups

Group keywords by topic and intent. Each group should map to one page on your site (and one ad group in Google Ads). Don't create separate pages for close variations — that splits your authority. Instead, cover the variations naturally on one strong page.

Your Best Keyword Source: Your Own Data

Google Search Console shows the exact searches already bringing you impressions, and the Google Ads search terms report shows what people typed before clicking your ads. Both reveal real language from real customers that no tool will show you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free keyword research tool in South Africa?

Google Keyword Planner (inside Google Ads) and Google Search Console are the most reliable free tools, especially when set to South Africa. Search Console shows the exact searches already reaching your site.

How do I choose which keywords to target?

Prioritise keywords with buying intent that match services you offer in areas you serve, and that you can realistically compete for, rather than chasing the highest volumes.

Should I create a separate page for every keyword?

No. Group closely related keywords and cover them on one strong page. Separate pages for minor variations split your authority.
