Website Design & Development in South Africa

We design and build fast, mobile-first websites for South African businesses — built to rank on Google, load quickly on mobile data and turn visitors into enquiries. From five-page business sites to landing pages and online stores.

Your website is where every other channel sends people. Ads, SEO, social media and referrals all end there, so a slow, confusing or outdated site wastes the money spent getting visitors to it. We build websites around one goal: turning the right visitors into enquiries and sales.

What We Build

Business websites

Professional sites for service businesses, typically five to fifteen pages, with clear service pages, proof and easy ways to get in touch.

Landing pages

Focused pages for Google Ads and Meta campaigns, built to match the ad and convert paid traffic.

E-commerce stores

Online stores with South African payment gateways, delivery options and product feeds ready for Google Shopping.

Redesigns

Rebuilding outdated sites without losing Google rankings — with URL mapping, redirects and tracking carried across.

Every Website Includes

  • Mobile-first design that works on mid-range phones and mobile data.
  • Speed optimisation — compressed images, lean code and fast hosting.
  • SEO foundations — page structure, titles, metadata, schema markup and a sitemap, set up properly from day one.
  • Conversion tracking — GA4, Google Ads and Meta tracking for forms, calls and WhatsApp clicks.
  • Clear calls to action on every page, with phone and WhatsApp options.
  • POPIA basics — privacy notice and consent-aware forms.
  • Ownership — the domain, hosting account and website are yours.

How a Website Project Works

  1. Discovery. We learn about your business, customers and competitors, and agree the pages, features and goals.
  2. Structure and content. We plan the site map and page content, including the searches each page should rank for.
  3. Design. You review designs for key pages before we build.
  4. Build and test. We develop the site, connect forms and tracking, and test on real phones.
  5. Launch. Redirects, Search Console and analytics checked, then live.

Most small business websites take four to eight weeks, depending mainly on how quickly content and approvals come together.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost with Nines Media?

Business websites start from R15,000, quoted per project based on the number of pages, design, content and functionality. E-commerce and custom features cost more.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small business websites take four to eight weeks. Content preparation and approvals are usually the biggest factors in the timeline.

Will my new website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site includes SEO foundations: page structure, titles and descriptions, schema markup, a sitemap, fast loading and mobile-first design. Ongoing SEO is available separately.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, hosting account and website belong to you.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We redesign sites while protecting existing Google rankings by mapping old URLs to new ones and setting up redirects.
