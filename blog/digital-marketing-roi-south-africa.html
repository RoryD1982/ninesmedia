Digital Marketing ROI in South Africa: What Results Should You Expect?

By Brandon Howard2026-10-128 min read

One of the most common questions South African business owners ask before investing in digital marketing is: "What will I get back?" It's the right question — and this guide answers it with real benchmarks, not vague promises.

How to Think About Digital Marketing ROI

ROI in digital marketing is measured differently depending on the channel and your business model. The key metrics are:

  • Cost Per Lead (CPL): How much you spend to generate one enquiry
  • Lead-to-Client Conversion Rate: What percentage of enquiries become paying clients
  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV): How much a new client is worth to your business over time
  • Return on Ad Spend (ROAS): Revenue generated per rand of ad spend (mainly relevant for e-commerce)

SEO ROI Timeline

SEO ROI is slower to materialise but compounding. A business that invests R8,000/month in SEO for 12 months and achieves first-page rankings for 10 high-intent keywords may generate 100–300 organic leads per month at effectively zero marginal cost per lead thereafter. The ROI calculation changes dramatically in year 2 and 3.

Social Media ROI

Organic social media ROI is notoriously difficult to measure directly. The more measurable metric is paid social (Meta Ads): a well-run SA Facebook/Instagram campaign typically generates leads at R80–R300 CPL for consumer-facing services and R200–R600 CPL for B2B.

Email Marketing ROI

Email consistently delivers the highest ROI of any digital marketing channel globally — typically R36–R42 return for every R1 spent. For SA businesses with an existing contact database, email marketing is often the fastest way to generate revenue with minimal spend.

The compounding argument: Digital marketing ROI improves significantly over time. A business that starts Google Ads and SEO simultaneously typically sees paid ads generating leads in weeks and SEO generating comparable volume at a lower CPL within 12–18 months — while the paid campaign has collected enough data to also improve its efficiency.

A Worked ROI Example

A Johannesburg home renovation company spends R10,000 a month on Google Ads plus R5,000 on management. The campaign produces 40 leads; 6 become jobs with an average gross profit of R18,000.

  • Total cost: R15,000
  • Gross profit from new jobs: 6 × R18,000 = R108,000
  • ROI = (R108,000 − R15,000) ÷ R15,000 = 620%

Use gross profit rather than revenue, otherwise ROI looks better than it really is. And include management and creative costs, not just ad spend.

Customer Lifetime Value Changes Everything

For businesses with repeat customers — accountants, dentists, gyms, subscription products — the first sale understates what marketing is worth. If an accounting client stays for five years, the value of acquiring them is the profit over those five years, not one month's fee. Knowing lifetime value tells you how much you can afford to pay for a new customer.

How to Improve Digital Marketing ROI

  • Improve conversion rates on landing pages before buying more traffic. Doubling conversion halves cost per lead.
  • Follow up leads faster. Slow responses waste leads you've already paid for.
  • Cut what doesn't convert — search terms, audiences, placements and ads with spend but no customers.
  • Feed sales data back into Google and Meta so they optimise for real customers, not just form fills.
  • Invest in channels that compound — SEO, email lists and reviews — alongside paid media.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate digital marketing ROI?

ROI = (gross profit from marketing − total marketing cost) ÷ total marketing cost. Include management, creative and tool costs, not just ad spend.

What is a good marketing ROI?

It depends on margins and customer lifetime value. As a rough guide, many businesses aim for at least 3:1 on revenue, but profitability after all costs is what matters.

How long until digital marketing shows ROI?

Paid search can show returns within one to three months. SEO and content usually take six to twelve months but keep producing leads without paying per click.
