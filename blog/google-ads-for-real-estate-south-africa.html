Google Ads for Real Estate Agents in South Africa | Win More Mandates

2026-10-137 min read

South African real estate is a high-value, high-competition market. Whether you're an independent agent, a franchise office or a boutique agency, Google Ads gives you a direct line to motivated buyers and sellers who are actively searching. Done correctly, it's one of the most accountable marketing channels available to real estate professionals.

Two Distinct Audiences: Buyers and Sellers

Real estate Google Ads serve two fundamentally different audiences with different intent signals and different campaign structures:

Buyer campaigns: People searching for properties. These drive traffic to your listings — use them to promote specific developments, suburbs or property types.

Seller / mandate campaigns: Property owners considering selling. These are the higher lifetime value lead — a seller mandate could be worth R50,000–R250,000+ in commission. Target searches like "sell my property [suburb]," "property valuation Cape Town," "what is my home worth Johannesburg."

Never mix these in the same campaign. Different intent, different ads, different landing pages.

Keyword Strategy for Real Estate

South African property searches are location-specific. Effective keyword targets:

Buyer keywords:

  • "3 bedroom house for sale [suburb]"
  • "Apartments to buy [city]"
  • "New developments [area]"

Seller keywords:

  • "Sell house [suburb]"
  • "Property agent [suburb]"
  • "Free property valuation [city]"
  • "How to sell my house in South Africa"

Use exact and phrase match types, and build comprehensive negative keyword lists to exclude rental-only searches, job searches and research queries.

Landing Pages: The Difference Between Good and Wasted Spend

The most common real estate Google Ads mistake is driving traffic to a generic homepage or a portal like Property24. You want traffic arriving at a landing page you control, with:

  • A headline matching the ad ("Free Property Valuation in Constantia — Book Online")
  • A simple form capturing name, number, property address and preferred contact time
  • Your agent credentials, sold properties and average days to sale
  • Testimonials from recent sellers or buyers
  • A local area map showing your coverage

Geo-Targeting for Estate Agents

Google Ads' geo-targeting allows you to show ads exclusively within specific suburbs, postcodes or radius areas. An agent working Claremont and Newlands doesn't need to pay for clicks from Durbanville. Tight geo-targeting reduces wasted spend dramatically in real estate, where location is everything.

Google Display for Property

Display remarketing — showing visual banner ads to people who've visited your website — works well in real estate's long consideration cycle. A buyer who viewed your listings last week sees your brand across news sites, email platforms and other websites until they're ready to enquire. Given real estate's typically long decision timeline, staying visible during the consideration phase pays off.

ROI example: A Johannesburg agent investing R8,000/month on Google Ads (management + spend) and generating 15 qualified seller enquiries per month, converting 2 into mandates at an average R80,000 commission, is seeing a 20x return on marketing spend. Not all campaigns achieve this — but well-managed real estate Google Ads are one of the highest-ROI marketing channels for agents willing to invest in quality execution.

Measuring Real Estate Google Ads Performance

Track: cost per lead (form submission or phone call), lead-to-appointment rate, appointment-to-mandate rate and revenue per mandate. The further down the funnel you can measure, the better you can optimise. Most agents only track cost per click — that's like judging a salesperson by how many calls they make rather than how many they close.
