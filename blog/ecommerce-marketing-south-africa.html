Digital Marketing for E-Commerce in South Africa: What Actually Drives Sales

Nines Media Team Updated 9 October 2026 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • The core engine: Google Shopping/Performance Max for demand capture + Meta for discovery + email/SMS for repeat sales.
  • Your product feed is your ad creative on Google — titles, images and attributes drive performance.
  • South African shoppers want trust signals: clear delivery costs and times, local payment options, easy returns and reviews.
  • Track ROAS against margin, plus repeat purchase rate and customer lifetime value.

South African online shopping keeps growing, and so does the competition for every click. The stores that win don't just buy more traffic. They build a system that captures demand, creates new demand, converts visitors efficiently and brings customers back.

1. Capture demand with Google Shopping and Performance Max

When someone searches "running shoes for women" or "air fryer 5L", Google Shopping shows product images, prices and store names. For most South African stores, this is the highest-intent traffic available.

  • Set up Google Merchant Center and enable free listings.
  • Optimise your product feed: descriptive titles (brand, product type, key attributes), high-quality images, accurate prices and stock.
  • Run Performance Max or Standard Shopping campaigns, segmented by margin or best sellers.
  • Exclude or separate branded search so you can see true new-customer performance.

2. Create demand with Meta and TikTok

Social ads find people who weren't searching yet. Meta Advantage+ Shopping campaigns combine prospecting and retargeting and work well with a healthy pixel and catalogue. Use:

  • Short vertical videos showing the product in use.
  • User-generated content and reviews.
  • Catalogue ads for dynamic retargeting.

See Facebook ads costs in South Africa and TikTok ads in South Africa.

3. Convert more visitors

Extra traffic is expensive; extra conversion rate is free. Focus on:

  • Speed — fast mobile pages on South African networks.
  • Clear delivery information — costs, couriers, timelines and free-delivery thresholds on product pages.
  • Local payment options — card, instant EFT, wallets and buy-now-pay-later options through gateways such as PayFast, Peach Payments or Yoco.
  • Trust signals — reviews, secure checkout badges, returns policy, physical address and contact details.
  • Simple checkout — guest checkout, fewer fields, autofill.
  • WhatsApp support for pre-purchase questions.

4. Bring customers back with email and SMS

Acquiring a customer is expensive; selling to them again is cheap. Set up automated flows:

  • Welcome series for new subscribers.
  • Abandoned cart and browse reminders.
  • Post-purchase thank-you, review requests and cross-sells.
  • Win-back campaigns for lapsed customers.

Collect consent properly under POPIA, and make unsubscribing easy.

5. Use search and content for long-term growth

Category pages, buying guides and comparison content capture organic traffic at a lower cost over time. Optimise category and product pages for the terms people search, and add structured data for products and reviews.

Metrics that matter

Metric Why it matters
ROAS (by campaign) Revenue per rand of ad spend
Break-even ROAS 1 ÷ gross margin — your floor
Conversion rate Site and checkout effectiveness
Average order value Lifts revenue per visitor
New customer CAC Cost to win a first purchase
Repeat purchase rate / LTV Long-term profitability

Common mistakes

  • Running one Performance Max campaign for every product regardless of margin.
  • Poor product feeds with vague titles.
  • Hiding delivery costs until checkout.
  • No email flows — leaving repeat revenue untouched.
  • Judging Meta purely on last-click sales.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best marketing channel for e-commerce in South Africa?

Most online stores rely on a combination: Google Shopping and Performance Max to capture product searches, Meta Advantage+ Shopping campaigns to reach new buyers, and email and SMS to drive repeat purchases.

How do I advertise my online store on Google Shopping?

Create a Google Merchant Center account, upload a product feed with accurate titles, prices, images and availability, link it to Google Ads, and run Shopping or Performance Max campaigns. Free listings are also available.

What is a good conversion rate for a South African online store?

Many stores convert between about 1% and 3% of visitors, with higher rates for repeat customers and branded traffic. Improving trust signals, speed and checkout usually lifts conversion more than extra traffic.

How much should an online store spend on ads?

Work from your margins. Calculate break-even ROAS (1 ÷ gross margin) and the cost you can afford per new customer, then scale budgets on campaigns beating that target.

Should I sell on Takealot as well as my own store?

Marketplaces like Takealot and Amazon.co.za add reach and trust, but come with fees and less control over the customer relationship. Many brands use them alongside their own store.
