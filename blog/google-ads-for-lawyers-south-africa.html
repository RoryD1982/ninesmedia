Google Ads for Lawyers in South Africa: A Complete Guide

By Brandon Howard 2026-10-20 6 min read

Legal services is one of the highest-value and most competitive Google Ads categories in South Africa. Keywords like "divorce attorney Johannesburg" or "labour lawyer Cape Town" can cost R60–R120 per click — but a single converted client can be worth R15,000–R150,000+ in fees. For law firms that get the strategy right, Google Ads delivers an ROI that few other marketing channels can match.

Can South African Lawyers Advertise on Google?

Yes. The Legal Practice Act and the Law Society rules on advertising have been liberalised significantly. South African attorneys can now advertise their services online, including through Google Ads, provided the advertising is not misleading, does not make guarantees about outcomes, and complies with general professional conduct rules. Google itself has no special restrictions on legal advertising in South Africa beyond its standard policies.

What Budget Do Law Firms Need?

For a single-practice or small firm, a starting budget of R10,000–R20,000/month in ad spend is the minimum to generate meaningful volume in competitive categories. Larger firms or those in high-CPC practice areas should budget R25,000–R60,000/month in ad spend for substantive results.

Add professional PPC management (R5,000–R12,000/month) to ensure campaigns are actively optimised. A poorly managed legal campaign at these CPCs burns budget rapidly.

Landing Page Requirements for Legal Google Ads

The landing page is where most legal Google Ads campaigns fail. Key requirements:

  • Practice-area specific page — not a generic "contact us" page
  • Attorney bio and credentials prominently featured
  • Clear statement of what the consultation includes (free 30-minute call, for example)
  • Phone number clickable on mobile (many legal enquiries happen by call, not form)
  • Client testimonials — social proof is critical in legal services
  • No outcome guarantees in copy (professionally inappropriate and often ineffective)

Call Tracking: Essential for Legal Firms

Most legal enquiries come via phone call, not a website form. Without call tracking, you have no idea which keywords and ads are driving actual consultations. Use a call tracking solution (CallRail, WhatConverts, or Google's native call tracking) to attribute calls to specific campaigns and keywords. This data is what allows you to optimise toward actual revenue rather than just clicks.

What Results Can Legal Firms Expect?

With a well-managed campaign and a strong landing page:

  • Cost per consultation enquiry: R800–R3,500 depending on practice area and city
  • Consultation to retained client rate: 20–40% for most practice areas
  • Effective cost per new client: R2,000–R15,000

For most legal services, this represents a highly positive ROI — a single family law matter retained from a Google Ads lead can return 10–50x the cost of acquiring it.

Structure Campaigns by Practice Area

Legal searches differ hugely in value and competition. Run separate campaigns for each practice area — for example personal injury and RAF claims, divorce and family law, labour disputes, criminal defence, conveyancing, wills and estates, and commercial law — each with its own budget, keywords and landing page. This shows which areas bring profitable clients and stops one expensive area consuming the whole budget.

Intake Is Half the Campaign

Legal leads are expensive, so losing them is costly:

  • Answer calls during business hours, or use a professional answering service.
  • Reply to forms and WhatsApp messages within the hour.
  • Use a short intake checklist so every enquiry is qualified the same way.
  • Record the outcome of each lead (consultation booked, retained, not suitable) and feed it back to the campaign.

Stay Within the Rules

Advertising by legal practitioners must follow the Legal Practice Council's Code of Conduct, so keep ads factual: describe your services, location and how to get in touch, and avoid claims about guaranteed outcomes or being better than other practitioners. Google also restricts some legal-related ads. If in doubt, have your marketing reviewed against the LPC rules before it runs.

Improving Lead Quality

Add negative keywords for searches you don't want — "free legal advice", "legal aid", "law degree", "jobs", "template" — unless those fit your practice. Mention consultation fees or the type of matters you handle on your landing page to filter out poor-fit enquiries before they call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can law firms advertise on Google in South Africa?

Yes. Legal practitioners can advertise, but must follow the Legal Practice Council's Code of Conduct, keeping ads factual and avoiding guaranteed outcomes or claims of superiority.

How much should a law firm spend on Google Ads?

Legal keywords are among the most expensive in South Africa. Most firms need at least R8,000 to R20,000 a month in ad spend per practice area to generate consistent enquiries in major cities.

How do law firms improve Google Ads lead quality?

Use separate campaigns by practice area, add negative keywords, explain the matters you handle and consultation terms on landing pages, and qualify every enquiry consistently.
