Customer Acquisition Cost: How to Calculate and Lower It

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • CAC is the total cost of winning one new customer.
  • Compare CAC with customer lifetime value to know what you can afford to spend.
  • Better conversion and follow-up often lower CAC faster than cheaper clicks.

Customer acquisition cost (CAC) answers a simple question: how much does it cost to win one new customer? Knowing it lets you decide how much to spend on marketing, which channels to scale and whether your growth is profitable.

How to Calculate CAC

CAC = total sales and marketing cost ÷ number of new customers over the same period.

Include ad spend, agency or freelancer fees, marketing tools, content and creative costs — and, for a fuller picture, the cost of sales time spent on new business.

A Worked Example

A Johannesburg accounting practice spends R8,000 on Google Ads and R5,000 on management in a month — R13,000 in total. It gets 30 enquiries and signs 5 new clients.

  • Cost per lead: R13,000 ÷ 30 = about R433
  • CAC: R13,000 ÷ 5 = R2,600

If an average client pays R2,500 a month and stays for three years, R2,600 to acquire them is excellent value.

CAC vs Lifetime Value

Customer lifetime value (LTV) is the profit a customer brings over the whole relationship. A common rule of thumb is to aim for LTV of at least three times CAC, but the right ratio depends on your margins and cash flow. Businesses with repeat customers can afford higher CAC than one-off sales businesses.

Ways to Lower CAC

  1. Improve conversion rate. If your landing page converts twice as many visitors, CAC can fall dramatically without changing ad spend. See CRO.
  2. Follow up faster. Leads contacted quickly convert far better.
  3. Cut wasted spend. Negative keywords, better targeting and pausing poor channels. See our audit checklist.
  4. Grow referrals and reviews. Customers from referrals usually cost little to acquire.
  5. Invest in SEO. Organic traffic lowers CAC over time.
  6. Improve lead quality. Qualifying questions and better targeting reduce time spent on poor leads.

Track CAC by Channel

Calculate CAC separately for Google Ads, Meta, SEO and referrals where you can. You'll often find one channel is far more efficient than another. See marketing KPIs and digital marketing ROI.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate customer acquisition cost?

Divide total sales and marketing costs for a period by the number of new customers won in that period.

What is a good customer acquisition cost?

It depends on what a customer is worth. Many businesses aim for customer lifetime value of at least three times CAC.

How can I reduce customer acquisition cost?

Improve conversion rates, follow up leads faster, cut wasted ad spend, grow referrals and invest in SEO.
