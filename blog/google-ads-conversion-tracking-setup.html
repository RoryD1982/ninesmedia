Google Ads Conversion Tracking: How to Track Calls, Forms and WhatsApp

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Without accurate conversion tracking, Google Ads can't optimise for real enquiries — and you can't judge results.
  • Track all three ways South African customers get in touch: forms, calls and WhatsApp.
  • Only count genuine actions: a submitted form, not a button click.

Conversion tracking tells Google Ads which clicks turned into enquiries or sales. It's the foundation of every successful campaign: it lets automated bidding find more people like your customers, and it lets you see your real cost per lead. Yet many South African accounts we review track nothing, or track the wrong thing.

What to Track

ActionHow to track it
Form submissionsThank-you page visit, or a confirmed form-submit event via Google Tag Manager
Calls from adsCall assets with Google forwarding numbers (where available) or call reporting
Calls from your websitePhone number click events on mobile; call tracking software for full accuracy
WhatsApp clicksClick events on wa.me links or WhatsApp buttons
PurchasesE-commerce purchase tag with transaction value

Step-by-Step Setup

  1. Install Google Tag Manager on every page of your site. It lets you add and change tracking without editing code each time.
  2. Connect Google Analytics 4 and mark your important actions as key events. See our GA4 guide.
  3. Create conversion actions in Google Ads — either directly with the Google Ads tag, or by importing GA4 key events.
  4. Set up form tracking on a confirmed submission. If your form shows a thank-you message without changing page, use a form-submit trigger or a dedicated event.
  5. Track phone and WhatsApp clicks with click triggers on tel: and wa.me links.
  6. Choose primary conversions — the actions bidding should optimise for. Keep minor actions (like brochure downloads) as secondary.
  7. Test everything with Tag Assistant and a real test enquiry before launching.

Common Tracking Mistakes

  • Counting button clicks as leads, including failed or empty submissions.
  • Double counting the same conversion from both the Google Ads tag and a GA4 import.
  • Making page views a primary conversion, which teaches Google to find browsers, not buyers.
  • Ignoring calls in industries where most leads phone in.
  • Never checking the numbers against what your team actually receives.

Going Further: Lead Quality

Once basic tracking works, feed back what happened next. Importing qualified leads or sales from your CRM (offline conversion import) helps Google optimise for customers, not just form fills — particularly important for Performance Max and broad match.

Consent and POPIA

Tracking collects personal information, so explain it in your privacy notice and respect visitors' choices. Google's Consent Mode lets tags adjust their behaviour based on consent. See POPIA and marketing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is conversion tracking important in Google Ads?

It lets Google's bidding optimise for real enquiries and sales, and lets you calculate your cost per lead and return on ad spend. Without it, you're judging campaigns on clicks.

Can Google Ads track WhatsApp leads?

Yes. You can track clicks on WhatsApp buttons and wa.me links as conversions using Google Tag Manager or GA4 events.

Should I import conversions from GA4 or use the Google Ads tag?

Either works. The important thing is not to count the same action twice, and to make sure the primary conversion is a genuine lead or sale.
