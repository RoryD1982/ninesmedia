Choosing an Agency

Cheap vs Affordable Digital Marketing: How to Tell the Difference

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Affordable means a smaller, honest scope; cheap usually means corners cut where you can't see them.
  • Ask who does the work, what's measured and who owns the accounts.
  • The cheapest quote often costs more once wasted ad spend is counted.

Every business wants value for money. But in digital marketing, the cheapest option often turns out to be the most expensive — because wasted ad spend and lost months cost more than the fee saved. The difference between cheap and affordable is what's included and how honestly it's described.

Cheap vs Affordable at a Glance

CheapAffordable
ScopePromises many channels for a tiny feeFocuses on one or two channels done well
TrackingReports clicks and impressionsTracks calls, forms and sales
AccountsAgency-owned; you can't see insideIn your name; full access
ContractLong lock-inMonth-to-month or short term
Who does the workUnclear, often outsourcedNamed person you can talk to
OptimisationSet and forgetRegular, documented changes

Questions That Reveal the Difference

  1. What exactly will you do each month?
  2. How will you track enquiries from calls and WhatsApp, not just forms?
  3. Will the ad account and analytics be in my name?
  4. Who will manage my account, and can I speak to them?
  5. What happens if results aren't good after three months?
  6. Is ad spend included in your price — and if so, how much goes to Google or Meta?

The Real Cost of Cheap

Imagine paying R1,500 a month for management and R5,000 in ads, but the campaign targets broad keywords with no negative keywords and no conversion tracking. Half the clicks could be irrelevant. That's R2,500 a month wasted — more than the management fee — plus months of lost opportunity.

What a Fair Low-Cost Package Looks Like

A fair entry package is narrow and specific: one channel, proper setup and tracking, regular optimisation, honest reporting and no lock-in. For example, our Google Ads Essentials covers Google Ads only, for ad spend of R3,000–R5,000, at R2,500 a month, and says plainly what it doesn't include. Frequently Asked Questions

Is cheap digital marketing worth it?

Rarely. Very low prices usually mean little optimisation, poor tracking and wasted ad spend. A focused, honestly scoped affordable package usually delivers far more value.

How do I know if an agency is cutting corners?

Warning signs include no access to your own accounts, reports without enquiries or sales, unclear deliverables, and long contracts.

What should an affordable package include?

One or two channels, proper setup and conversion tracking, regular optimisation, a clear monthly report, and accounts owned by you.
