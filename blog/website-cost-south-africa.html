How Much Does a Website Cost in South Africa? (2026 Prices)

By Brandon Howard Brandon — Nines Media 2026-08-20 8 min read

Website pricing in South Africa is notoriously opaque. One agency will quote R5,000 for a "business website"; another will quote R80,000 for something that looks superficially similar. This guide breaks down what different budgets actually deliver, what ongoing costs you need to plan for, and what any professional website should include regardless of price.

Website Cost Tiers in South Africa (2026)

TypeBudget RangeWhat You Get
DIY / Template (Wix, Squarespace)R0–R500/monthSelf-built, template-based. Fine for sole traders and early-stage businesses. Limited SEO and conversion capability.
Basic Business SiteR5,000–R18,000Professional design, 5–8 pages, mobile-responsive, contact form, basic SEO. WordPress or similar CMS.
Growth / Lead-Gen SiteR20,000–R60,000Custom design, conversion-focused layout, Google Analytics, Google Ads tracking, integrated CRM, speed-optimised.
E-commerceR25,000–R150,000Depends heavily on product count, payment gateway, and custom features. Shopify vs. WooCommerce vs. custom build.
Enterprise / CustomR150,000–R500,000+Custom development, integrations with internal systems, advanced functionality, dedicated project team.

What Every Website Should Include (Non-Negotiables)

  • Mobile-first design — over 70% of SA web traffic is mobile; if it doesn't work perfectly on a phone, it doesn't work
  • HTTPS / SSL certificate — Google flags sites without it; most hosts include it free
  • Google Analytics 4 — you need to know who's visiting and from where
  • Speed optimisation — a 1-second delay reduces conversions by 7%; South African mobile connections make this critical
  • Clear calls to action — every page should have one obvious next step
  • Basic on-page SEO — proper title tags, meta descriptions, and heading structure
  • Contact information and map/directions — especially for local businesses

The Real Cost of a Cheap Website

A R5,000 website that nobody can find, loads slowly on mobile, and has no clear call-to-action isn't an asset — it's a sunk cost. The cheapest website is almost never the best value. When evaluating quotes, ask: how will this site generate leads? What happens to the relationship after launch? Who do I call if it breaks? What SEO work is included?

How Much Does a 5-Page Website Cost in South Africa?

A five-page business website (typically Home, About, Services, a blog or projects page, and Contact) is the most common small-business build. In 2026 most South African quotes for this fall roughly into three bands:

Who builds itTypical priceWhat to expect
Freelancer using a templateR5,000 – R12,000Pre-built theme, your content dropped in, basic contact form
Small studio or agencyR12,000 – R30,000Custom design, copy guidance, on-page SEO, analytics and tracking
Conversion-focused buildR30,000 – R60,000Strategy, copywriting, landing pages, CRM and ad tracking integrations

Each extra page usually adds R1,000–R3,000 depending on whether it's built from an existing template or designed from scratch.

What Drives the Price of Website Design?

  • Custom design vs template. A bespoke design takes longer than adapting a theme, but fits your brand and conversion goals.
  • Copywriting. Many quotes assume you supply the words. Professional copy can add R3,000–R10,000.
  • Functionality. Booking systems, quote calculators, member areas and integrations add development time.
  • E-commerce. Product setup, payment gateways (PayFast, Yoco, Peach Payments) and shipping rules add significantly.
  • SEO and speed. Proper page structure, metadata, schema and performance work cost a little more upfront and pay back in rankings.

Website Cost Per Month

Beyond the build, budget for running costs. A typical small-business site costs roughly R150–R700 a month for hosting and domain, plus R500–R2,000 a month if you want someone handling updates, backups and security. Website builders like Wix or Squarespace bundle hosting into a subscription instead, usually from a few hundred rand a month.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a basic website cost in South Africa?

A basic professional business website in South Africa typically costs R5,000 to R18,000 in 2026, depending on whether it's template-based or custom designed and how many pages it has.

How much is a 5-page website in South Africa?

Most quotes for a five-page business website fall between about R5,000 (freelancer with a template) and R30,000 (agency with custom design, SEO and tracking). Conversion-focused builds with copywriting and integrations can cost R30,000 to R60,000.

What does a website cost per month?

Hosting and domain usually cost R150 to R700 a month for a small business. Ongoing maintenance, updates and security typically add R500 to R2,000 a month if outsourced.

How much does an e-commerce website cost in South Africa?

E-commerce sites typically cost R25,000 to R150,000 depending on product count, payment gateway, platform (Shopify, WooCommerce or custom) and custom features.
