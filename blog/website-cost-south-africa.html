How Much Does a Website Cost in South Africa? A Transparent Pricing Guide for 2026

Nines Media Team Updated 9 October 2026 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Template/DIY sites cost R3,000–R15,000, professional business sites R15,000–R60,000, and e-commerce stores R30,000–R150,000+.
  • Ongoing costs (domain, hosting, maintenance, updates) typically add R150–R2,500 per month.
  • Price is driven by custom design, number of pages, copywriting, integrations and how much the site must do to generate leads or sales.
  • Every professional build should include mobile optimisation, speed, basic SEO and clear calls to action.

One of the most common questions South African business owners ask when planning a website is: what should this cost? The honest answer is anything from R3,000 to R500,000 — and the difference is not always obvious from the outside. This guide breaks down what different types of websites cost in South Africa, what drives the price, and the running costs people forget to budget for.

Website prices in South Africa at a glance

Website type Typical cost Timeline Best for
DIY / website builder R0–R5,000 + monthly plan Days Testing an idea, side businesses
Template site (freelancer) R3,000–R15,000 1–3 weeks Basic online presence
Professional business website R15,000–R60,000 3–6 weeks SMEs that want leads from their site
E-commerce store R30,000–R150,000+ 4–12 weeks Selling products online
Custom platform / web app R150,000–R500,000+ 3–9 months Portals, bookings, complex integrations

Template and DIY websites: R3,000–R15,000

Basic WordPress, Wix or Squarespace sites built from a template with light customisation. They are fine if you simply need to exist online. The trade-offs are significant: generic design that looks like thousands of other sites, slower load times, weak on-page SEO and little thought given to conversion. Many businesses outgrow them within two years.

Professional business websites: R15,000–R60,000

This is where most established South African SMEs should be. At this level the website is designed around your audience and goals, with:

  • Custom design that reflects your brand.
  • Well-written copy structured around what customers search for.
  • Fast loading on mobile networks — important in South Africa, where many visitors browse on mobile data.
  • On-page SEO, structured data and Google Analytics 4 set up correctly.
  • Clear calls to action: click-to-call, WhatsApp, quote forms and booking.
  • A content management system you can update yourself.

A good business website should pay for itself within six to twelve months through new enquiries if your average sale is meaningful.

E-commerce websites: R30,000–R150,000+

Online stores need product catalogues, local payment gateways such as PayFast, Peach Payments or Yoco, shipping rules for South African couriers, stock management and an optimised checkout. Shopify or WooCommerce builds for stores with fewer than about 500 products usually sit at the lower end; custom integrations with ERP, accounting or warehouse systems push costs higher. For marketing an online store once it is built, see e-commerce marketing in South Africa.

What drives website cost

  • Custom design vs template — bespoke design takes more designer time.
  • Number of pages and templates — a five-page site and a fifty-page site are different projects.
  • Copywriting — professional copy is often the most valuable and most overlooked line item.
  • Functionality — bookings, quote calculators, member areas, multilingual content and integrations.
  • Photography and video — original imagery outperforms stock but costs more.
  • SEO and migration — redirecting old URLs properly protects existing rankings.

Ongoing website costs

Item Typical cost
.co.za domain ~R100–R200 per year
Hosting ~R100–R1,000 per month
SSL certificate Often free with hosting
Maintenance (updates, backups, security) ~R500–R2,500 per month
Premium plugins / apps Varies; common on WordPress and Shopify

What every website build should include

Regardless of budget, insist on:

  1. Mobile-first design that has been tested on real phones.
  2. Page speed optimisation — aim for pages to load in under about three seconds on mobile.
  3. Basic on-page SEO: titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text and a sitemap.
  4. Conversion tracking for forms, calls and WhatsApp clicks.
  5. Ownership of your domain, hosting account and website files.
  6. Training on how to update content yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a basic website cost in South Africa?

A simple template website on a platform like WordPress or Wix typically costs R3,000–R15,000 built by a freelancer, or less if you build it yourself. A professionally designed small business website with custom design, copy and SEO usually costs R15,000–R60,000.

How much does an e-commerce website cost in South Africa?

Most Shopify or WooCommerce stores cost R30,000–R150,000 depending on product numbers, custom design, payment gateways such as PayFast or Peach Payments, and integrations with stock, courier or accounting systems. Complex custom platforms cost more.

What are the monthly costs of running a website?

A .co.za domain costs roughly R100–R200 a year, hosting about R100–R1,000 a month depending on traffic and platform, and optional maintenance plans R500–R2,500 a month for updates, backups and security.

How long does it take to build a website?

A small business website typically takes three to six weeks; e-commerce builds four to twelve weeks. Delays usually come from content — copy, photos and product data — rather than development.
