How Much Does a Website Cost in South Africa? A Transparent Pricing Guide for 2026

Brandon — Nines Media 2026-08-20 8 min read

Website pricing in South Africa is notoriously opaque. One agency will quote R5,000 for a "business website"; another will quote R80,000 for something that looks superficially similar. This guide breaks down what different budgets actually deliver, what ongoing costs you need to plan for, and what any professional website should include regardless of price.

Website Cost Tiers in South Africa (2026)

TypeBudget RangeWhat You Get
DIY / Template (Wix, Squarespace)R0–R500/monthSelf-built, template-based. Fine for sole traders and early-stage businesses. Limited SEO and conversion capability.
Basic Business SiteR5,000–R18,000Professional design, 5–8 pages, mobile-responsive, contact form, basic SEO. WordPress or similar CMS.
Growth / Lead-Gen SiteR20,000–R60,000Custom design, conversion-focused layout, Google Analytics, Google Ads tracking, integrated CRM, speed-optimised.
E-commerceR25,000–R150,000Depends heavily on product count, payment gateway, and custom features. Shopify vs. WooCommerce vs. custom build.
Enterprise / CustomR150,000–R500,000+Custom development, integrations with internal systems, advanced functionality, dedicated project team.

What Every Website Should Include (Non-Negotiables)

  • Mobile-first design — over 70% of SA web traffic is mobile; if it doesn't work perfectly on a phone, it doesn't work
  • HTTPS / SSL certificate — Google flags sites without it; most hosts include it free
  • Google Analytics 4 — you need to know who's visiting and from where
  • Speed optimisation — a 1-second delay reduces conversions by 7%; South African mobile connections make this critical
  • Clear calls to action — every page should have one obvious next step
  • Basic on-page SEO — proper title tags, meta descriptions, and heading structure
  • Contact information and map/directions — especially for local businesses

Ongoing Costs to Budget For

Cost ItemAnnual Cost (ZAR)Notes
Domain name (.co.za)R150–R300Renewed annually; ZANIC registered domains preferred for SA
Domain name (.com)R200–R400Register both if possible
Web hostingR1,200–R6,000Shared hosting is fine for most SMEs; avoid the cheapest options
WordPress maintenanceR500–R2,000/monthPlugin updates, security patches, backups
Content updatesR500–R3,000/monthIf you're outsourcing blog posts or page updates

The Real Cost of a Cheap Website

A R5,000 website that nobody can find, loads slowly on mobile, and has no clear call-to-action isn't an asset — it's a sunk cost. The cheapest website is almost never the best value. When evaluating quotes, ask: how will this site generate leads? What happens to the relationship after launch? Who do I call if it breaks? What SEO work is included?
