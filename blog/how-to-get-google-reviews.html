How to Get More Google Reviews (Without Breaking the Rules)

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • The best way to get reviews is to ask every happy customer, at the right moment, with a direct link.
  • Google prohibits incentivised reviews, fake reviews and only asking happy customers (review gating).
  • Reply to every review — including negative ones — professionally.

For local businesses, Google reviews influence both rankings in the map pack and whether people choose you once they find you. Most businesses have far fewer reviews than satisfied customers, simply because they never ask. Here's how to build a steady flow of genuine reviews.

When to Ask

Ask at the moment of highest satisfaction: when a job is finished and the customer says thank you, when a problem is solved, when a product arrives. Asking weeks later produces far fewer reviews.

How to Ask

  1. Get your review link. In your Google Business Profile, use the "Ask for reviews" option to copy a short link.
  2. Send it by WhatsApp. In South Africa, WhatsApp gets far faster responses than email.
  3. Make it personal. Use the customer's name and mention the job.
  4. Make it easy. One link, one sentence, no instructions to search for you.
  5. Remind once if there's no response after a few days — then leave it.

A Simple WhatsApp Template

"Hi Thandi, thanks again for choosing us for your geyser replacement. If you have a minute, we'd really appreciate an honest Google review — it helps other people in Randburg find us: [link]. Thank you! — Pieter, ABC Plumbing"

Build It Into Your Process

  • Add "send review link" to your job completion checklist.
  • Include the link in invoices and follow-up emails.
  • Put a QR code at your counter or reception.
  • Train staff to ask naturally when customers express thanks.

What Google Doesn't Allow

  • Incentives — discounts, gifts or entries into draws in exchange for reviews.
  • Review gating — only asking customers you think will leave positive reviews, or filtering unhappy customers away first.
  • Fake reviews — from staff, family, or paid services.
  • Reviews written on behalf of customers.

Breaking these rules can lead to reviews being removed or your profile being restricted — and South African consumer law also prohibits misleading representations.

Responding to Reviews

Reply to every review. Thank positive reviewers specifically. For negative reviews, respond calmly, acknowledge the issue, avoid arguing in public and offer to resolve it offline. In healthcare and professional services, never confirm someone is a client or discuss their case publicly.

Why Reviews Matter for AI Search Too

AI assistants that recommend local businesses often draw on reviews and reputation signals across the web. A steady stream of genuine reviews supports your visibility there as well. See getting recommended by AI search and our local SEO guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I ask customers for Google reviews?

Send a personal message with your direct review link straight after a positive experience, ideally by WhatsApp, and remind once if needed.

Can I offer a discount for Google reviews?

No. Google prohibits incentivised reviews, including discounts, gifts and competition entries.

Should I reply to negative Google reviews?

Yes, calmly and professionally. Acknowledge the issue and offer to resolve it offline, without arguing publicly.
