Branding & Brand Identity Design

A strong brand makes every other piece of marketing work harder. Ads get recognised, websites feel trustworthy, and customers remember you when they're ready to buy. Our branding work starts with strategy — who you're for and why you're different — and turns it into a visual identity that's consistent everywhere.

What's Included in a Brand Identity

Logo & brand mark

A primary logo, simplified mark and variations for dark and light backgrounds, digital and print.

Colour & typography

A colour palette and type system chosen for legibility, accessibility and personality.

Brand guidelines

A brand guide PDF showing how to use your logo, colours, fonts and imagery correctly.

Applications

Social media templates, ad layouts, stationery, signage and packaging, depending on scope.

Brand Strategy First

Before designing anything, we work out the foundations: your audience, what makes you different, your personality and how you want to be perceived compared with competitors. That strategy guides every design decision, so the result isn't just attractive — it's right for your market. Read branding vs marketing and how to build a brand identity for more on our approach.

How a Branding Project Works

  1. Discovery workshop — your business, customers, competitors and goals.
  2. Strategy — positioning, personality and the key messages your brand needs to carry.
  3. Concepts — initial logo and identity directions for you to review.
  4. Refinement — developing the chosen direction into a full identity system.
  5. Guidelines and files — final logo files in every format and your brand guide.

Rebrands and Brand Refreshes

Not every business needs a completely new identity. A refresh modernises your existing logo and visual style while keeping the recognition you've built. A full rebrand makes sense when your business has changed direction, merged, or outgrown a brand that no longer fits. We'll recommend which you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a brand identity cost in South Africa?

Nines Media brand identity packages start from R8,000 for a logo, brand mark, colour palette, typography and brand guide. Larger projects with strategy, applications or a full rebrand are quoted individually.

What is included in a brand identity package?

Typically a primary logo and variations, a brand mark, colour palette, typography and a brand guide showing how to use them. Templates, stationery and signage can be added.

How long does a branding project take?

Most brand identity projects take three to six weeks, depending on the number of concept rounds and how quickly feedback is given.

Should I rebrand or refresh my brand?

Refresh if your brand still fits your business but looks dated. Rebrand if your business has changed direction or the brand no longer reflects who you serve.
