Digital Marketing for Solar Energy Companies in South Africa | Get More Installations

2026-10-137 min read

Loadshedding transformed South Africa's solar market, and even as Eskom stabilises, solar adoption is accelerating — driven by rising electricity costs, environmental awareness and energy security concerns. For solar installation companies, the demand is there. The challenge is standing out in an increasingly crowded market and converting interest into signed contracts.

The Solar Buyer's Journey

Solar is a considered, high-value purchase — typically R80,000–R300,000+ for a residential system. The buyer's journey is long: research phase (weeks to months), comparison phase, shortlisting and quotes, then decision. Your digital marketing needs to be present at every stage, not just when someone is ready to buy.

SEO: Capture High-Intent Research Traffic

Solar buyers do extensive research before reaching out. They search for information about system sizing, battery types, inverters, costs and payback periods. A solar company with comprehensive, helpful content on these topics earns both the trust and the organic traffic that converts to leads.

High-value SEO targets:

  • "Solar system cost South Africa 2026"
  • "Best solar panels South Africa"
  • "Solar battery storage South Africa"
  • "Solar installer [city]"
  • "How much solar do I need for [appliance] South Africa"
  • "Solar payback period South Africa"

Create a comprehensive resources section: system sizing calculators, savings estimators, battery comparison guides, financing options. These resources attract and educate buyers while building the authority that drives rankings.

Google Ads for Solar Installers

Solar Google Ads work best for capturing buyers who are actively in the comparison and decision phases. Target:

  • "Solar installation [city]"
  • "Solar installer near me"
  • "Get solar quotes South Africa"
  • "Commercial solar system South Africa"

Given the high ticket value, a cost per lead of R500–R2,000 can still represent excellent ROI. Build dedicated landing pages per location and system type (residential, commercial, agricultural).

Facebook and Instagram Ads for Solar

Facebook's targeting allows you to reach homeowners in specific income brackets, specific suburbs and specific property types — precisely the profile of a potential solar buyer. Strategies that work:

  • Lead generation campaigns — in-app forms with qualifying questions (property type, monthly electricity spend, ownership status) to pre-qualify leads
  • Video campaigns — showing before/after electricity bills, installation footage and customer testimonials
  • Retargeting — reaching people who visited your website during their research phase

Building Trust in a Crowded Market

Post-loadshedding, hundreds of solar companies entered the South African market. Many were under-capitalised, poorly equipped or outright fraudulent. Buyers are now more cautious. Your digital presence must communicate credibility:

  • Registration with SAPVIA (South African Photovoltaic Industry Association)
  • COC (Certificate of Compliance) process explained clearly
  • SSEG (Small Scale Embedded Generation) registration assistance
  • Installer accreditation: SAIEE, NERSA registration as applicable
  • Verified Google reviews with real customer names and system details
  • Case studies with actual system sizes, savings and payback periods
Financing messaging matters: "From R1,200/month" or "pay less than your electricity bill from day one" messaging significantly increases conversion rates for solar leads. Feature your financing partners (e.g. Absa, Nedbank solar financing) prominently on landing pages.

Post-Loadshedding Market: What's Changed

With Eskom more stable in 2026, pure loadshedding urgency has waned. The new selling proposition is: rising electricity tariffs, energy independence, green building requirements and commercial power quality. Adjust your messaging accordingly — buyers who waited out loadshedding still have compelling economic reasons to go solar.
