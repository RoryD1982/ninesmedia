Should You Outsource Digital Marketing? A Guide for SA Business Owners

By Brandon Howard2026-10-127 min read

It's a question every growing South African business eventually faces: should we hire in-house, or outsource to a digital marketing agency? The right answer depends on your budget, growth stage, and how central digital is to your strategy. Here's a clear-eyed breakdown.

The Case for Outsourcing

  • Access to specialists: A good agency gives you access to a Google Ads specialist, an SEO strategist, a content writer, a designer, and a data analyst — without hiring five people
  • Speed: An experienced agency can have a Google Ads campaign running in days; recruiting, onboarding, and ramping a new employee takes months
  • No training cost: Digital platforms change constantly. Agencies stay current; keeping an in-house employee up to date requires ongoing training investment
  • Accountability: Agencies live and die by results. An employee who underperforms is harder to replace than an agency that misses targets

The Case for In-House

  • Brand knowledge: An in-house team lives and breathes your brand, culture, and customers
  • Speed on content: Day-to-day social media content and real-time community management is often better handled internally
  • Cost at scale: At high enough spend levels (R100k+/month in ad spend), the management fee percentage a agency charges can exceed the salary cost of an in-house specialist

The Hybrid Model (Most Common for SA Growth Businesses)

The most effective arrangement for many South African SMEs is a hybrid model: an agency manages strategic channels (Google Ads, SEO, paid social) while an internal marketing coordinator handles brand content, social media posting, and communications. This combines specialist expertise with brand authenticity.

Key question: What's the cost of getting it wrong? Hiring the wrong in-house person for 6 months and then having to start over is expensive. A trial month with an agency is a faster, lower-risk way to test capability and fit.
