Microsoft Advertising (Bing Ads) in South Africa: Is It Worth It?

By Brandon Howard 3 November 2026 4 min read
Key takeaways
  • Microsoft Advertising launched in South Africa in 2022, so local advertisers can run search ads on Bing.
  • Its audience is smaller than Google's but can be cheaper per click and skews towards desktop and work users.
  • Test it after Google Ads is working well — import your best campaigns and compare cost per lead.

Google dominates search in South Africa, so most businesses never consider Microsoft Advertising (formerly Bing Ads). But Microsoft expanded its advertising platform to South Africa in 2022, and for some businesses it's a useful, often cheaper, extra source of leads.

What Microsoft Advertising Is

Microsoft Advertising places search ads on Bing and across Microsoft's network, which includes partner search sites. It works much like Google Ads: you bid on keywords, write text ads and pay per click. Microsoft also integrates ads into some of its AI-powered search experiences.

Who It Can Work For

  • B2B businesses. Bing is the default search engine on many work computers using Microsoft Edge and Windows, so it can reach professionals during office hours.
  • Businesses targeting older or desktop-heavy audiences.
  • Advertisers whose Google campaigns are already maxed out on search volume and want additional reach.

Pros and Cons

ProsCons
Less competition can mean lower cost per clickMuch smaller search volume than Google
Easy to import Google Ads campaignsFewer conversions, so automated bidding learns more slowly
Reaches work and desktop usersPartner network traffic quality can vary — monitor placements

How to Test It

  1. Make sure your Google Ads campaigns and conversion tracking are working well first.
  2. Use Microsoft Advertising's import tool to copy your best-performing Google campaigns.
  3. Install the Microsoft UET tag on your site for conversion tracking.
  4. Check location targeting and network settings after import — don't assume they copied exactly as you want.
  5. Run for at least a month with a modest budget, then compare cost per lead with Google.

Should You Bother?

For most South African small businesses, Google Ads should come first, because that's where most searches happen. Once Google is profitable and limited by search volume — or if you're B2B — Microsoft Advertising is a low-risk test. Bing's search index also matters for visibility in some AI assistants, so make sure your site is set up in Bing Webmaster Tools regardless. See getting found in AI search.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Microsoft Advertising available in South Africa?

Yes. Microsoft expanded Microsoft Advertising to South Africa in 2022, so local businesses can run search ads on Bing and Microsoft's network.

Are Bing ads cheaper than Google Ads?

Often per click, because there's less competition, but search volume is much smaller. Compare cost per lead rather than cost per click.

Can I copy my Google Ads campaigns to Microsoft Advertising?

Yes. Microsoft provides an import tool. Review settings such as location targeting and networks after importing.
