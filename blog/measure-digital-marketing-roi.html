How to Measure Digital Marketing ROI: A Guide for South African Business Owners

Nines Media Team Updated 9 October 2026 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Marketing ROI = (revenue from marketing − marketing cost) ÷ marketing cost. Include agency fees and creative costs, not just ad spend.
  • You need four building blocks: conversion tracking, call/WhatsApp tracking, CRM data and consistent UTMs.
  • Report on cost per customer and revenue, not just clicks and leads.
  • Platform numbers overlap — use your CRM or sales data as the source of truth.

Ask most South African business owners what their marketing returns and you'll hear "we get lots of clicks" or "our followers are up." Those are activity metrics. ROI is about money: what you put in, and what came back.

The ROI formula

Marketing ROI = (Revenue attributable to marketing − Total marketing cost) ÷ Total marketing cost

Better still, use gross profit instead of revenue, since a sale at a 20% margin returns far less than the same sale at 60%.

Total marketing cost should include:

  • Ad spend (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok).
  • Agency or freelancer fees.
  • Creative production (design, video, photography).
  • Tools and software.
  • Internal staff time, if you want the full picture.

Example: a business spends R25,000 in a month (R15,000 ads, R10,000 fees) and wins clients worth R100,000 in gross profit. ROI = (100,000 − 25,000) ÷ 25,000 = 300%.

The metrics that lead to ROI

Level Metrics Why it matters
Activity Impressions, clicks, CTR Tells you if ads are seen and relevant
Conversion Leads, purchases, conversion rate, CPL/CPA Tells you if traffic turns into action
Business Customers, revenue, CAC, ROAS, LTV Tells you if marketing makes money

Good reporting moves up this table. If your reports stop at the first row, you're not measuring ROI.

Building the tracking you need

1. Conversion tracking

Set up GA4 key events and platform conversion tags for every meaningful action: forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks, bookings and purchases.

2. Calls and WhatsApp

For many South African businesses, most leads arrive by phone or WhatsApp. Track:

  • Calls from Google Ads (call assets with forwarding numbers).
  • Calls from your website (dynamic call tracking, or at minimum click-to-call events).
  • WhatsApp button clicks as events.

3. CRM integration

Your CRM (HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive or even a well-kept spreadsheet) should record the source of each lead and whether it became a customer. Feed qualified and won leads back to Google Ads and Meta so their algorithms optimise for real customers.

4. UTM tags

Tag every link in ads, emails, social bios and partner sites so GA4 attributes visits correctly.

Attribution: who gets the credit?

A customer might see a TikTok ad, click a Meta retargeting ad, then search your name on Google and buy. Each platform may claim the sale. Practical approaches:

  • Use your CRM or sales data as the source of truth for totals.
  • Use GA4's data-driven attribution to see how channels contribute.
  • Watch blended metrics — total marketing cost ÷ total new customers.
  • Run holdout tests occasionally: pause a channel in one region or period and measure the impact.

A simple monthly ROI report

  1. Total spend by channel (ads + fees).
  2. Leads or orders by channel.
  3. Cost per lead / acquisition by channel.
  4. Customers won and revenue (from CRM).
  5. Blended CAC and ROI.
  6. What changed this month and why.
  7. Recommendations for next month.

Common mistakes

  • Counting ad spend but ignoring fees and creative.
  • Judging SEO and content on one month of data.
  • Celebrating cheap leads without checking how many closed.
  • Leaving offline sales out of the picture.

When ROI takes time

Paid channels typically show measurable results within weeks; SEO and content compound over months. Read how long digital marketing takes to show results to set fair expectations.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate digital marketing ROI?

Subtract total marketing cost (ad spend, fees, creative, tools) from the revenue or gross profit generated by marketing, then divide by the marketing cost. For example, R100,000 profit from R25,000 spend gives an ROI of 300%.

What is a good ROI for digital marketing?

Any positive ROI on gross profit is making money, but most businesses aim for at least 2–3 times return on total marketing cost. It varies by margin, customer lifetime value and how mature your channels are.

Why don't my Google Ads and Facebook numbers match my sales?

Each platform uses its own attribution and often claims credit for the same sale. Tracking gaps from privacy settings also affect reporting. Compare platform data with your CRM and look at overall trends.

What should a monthly marketing report include?

Spend by channel, leads or sales, cost per lead or acquisition, revenue or pipeline, conversion rates, key changes made, and recommendations for next month.

How do I track phone calls and WhatsApp leads?

Use Google Ads call tracking for ad calls, call-tracking numbers for website calls, and track WhatsApp button clicks as GA4 events. Ask new customers how they found you and record it in your CRM.
