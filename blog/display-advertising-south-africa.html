Display Advertising in South Africa: Google Display Network Guide

By Brandon Howard2026-10-126 min read

Display advertising — banner ads, image ads, and responsive ads placed across millions of websites, apps, and YouTube — is a powerful tool for South African businesses looking to build brand awareness, stay top-of-mind, and retarget website visitors. Here's how it works and when to use it.

What Is the Google Display Network?

The Google Display Network (GDN) reaches over 90% of internet users worldwide through millions of websites, apps, and Google properties. In South Africa, it covers major news sites (News24, TimesLive, IOL), YouTube, Gmail, and thousands of niche websites. Display ads appear as banners, images, or responsive ad units that adapt to available ad space.

Display vs Search: When to Use Each

ChannelBest ForUser Intent
Search (Google Ads)Capturing existing demandHigh — actively searching
DisplayBuilding awareness, retargetingLow — passively browsing

Display is not a substitute for Search when leads are the primary goal. It excels at keeping your brand visible to people who've already shown interest (retargeting) or reaching new audiences before they start searching (awareness).

Display Targeting Options in South Africa

  • Demographic: Age, gender, household income
  • In-market audiences: People Google has identified as actively researching a category
  • Custom intent: Target people who've searched specific keywords on Google
  • Remarketing: Show ads to people who've visited your website or YouTube channel
  • Placement targeting: Choose specific websites or apps to appear on
  • Topic targeting: Appear on websites about specific topics (health, finance, property)

Display Advertising Benchmarks for SA

MetricSA Benchmark
Cost per thousand impressions (CPM)R5 – R40
Click-through rate (CTR)0.05% – 0.35%
Cost per click (CPC)R3 – R30

Retargeting: The Highest-ROI Display Use Case

Retargeting (or remarketing) — showing display ads exclusively to people who've already visited your website — is the most effective display application for most SA businesses. The audience has already demonstrated intent; the ads simply keep you visible while they continue their research. A well-configured retargeting campaign can generate leads at a significantly lower CPC than prospecting campaigns.

Creative tip: Display ads must capture attention in under a second. A clear logo, a bold headline, and a single CTA outperform complex, detailed banners. Test your display creative on a phone screen — that's where most SA users will see it.

Display Creative That Works

  • Use responsive display ads — upload several images, logos, headlines and descriptions, and Google builds ads for every placement size.
  • One message per ad. People glance at display ads for a second. Lead with the offer or benefit, not your company history.
  • Show the product or result clearly, with your logo visible.
  • Add video where you can — short videos often outperform static images on the network.

Exclude Low-Quality Placements

Left unchecked, display budgets can drift to mobile games, apps and low-quality sites that generate accidental clicks. Review the "where ads showed" report regularly and exclude poor placements, consider excluding mobile app categories for lead generation, and set frequency caps so the same person doesn't see your ad too often.

How to Measure Display Campaigns

Display rarely wins on last-click conversions, so judge it on the job you gave it:

  • Prospecting — reach, frequency, cost per thousand impressions, and growth in branded searches and direct visits.
  • Retargeting — conversions and cost per conversion, including view-through conversions (with caution).

Compare periods with and without display activity to see its real contribution.

Display or Meta?

For visual products and consumer audiences, Meta often outperforms Google Display on engagement and targeting. Display's strengths are reach across websites and YouTube, contextual targeting, and retargeting Google Search visitors. Many businesses use both. Frequently Asked Questions

Is display advertising worth it in South Africa?

Display works well for retargeting website visitors and building awareness at low cost per impression. For direct lead generation, Google Search usually performs better.

How much does Google Display advertising cost?

Display is usually much cheaper per click and per thousand impressions than Search, but conversion rates are lower, so judge it on cost per result for retargeting and on reach for awareness.

How do I stop display ads showing on bad websites?

Review placement reports regularly, exclude poor sites and app categories, use content exclusions, and set frequency caps.
