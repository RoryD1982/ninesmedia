How Much Do Facebook & Instagram Ads Cost in South Africa? (2026)

Brandon — Nines Media 2026-09-18 8 min read

Meta advertising — Facebook and Instagram — is one of the most cost-effective ways to reach South African consumers, but the question "how much does it cost?" doesn't have a single answer. This guide breaks down what you'll actually pay, why costs vary, and how to structure your budget for real results.

How Meta Charges You

Meta uses an auction system where advertisers bid for ad placements. You can bid on CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions), CPC (cost per click), or CPR (cost per result, such as a lead or purchase). For most South African campaigns, Meta optimises automatically toward your objective — if you're running a lead generation campaign, Meta will serve your ads to people most likely to submit a form.

What Drives Costs Up

Audience size: Targeting an audience under 50,000 people creates intense competition within that pool, driving up CPMs. Aim for 200,000–2,000,000 for most campaigns.

Creative quality: Meta's algorithm rewards ads people engage with. High CTR (click-through rate) reduces your effective CPM. An ad with a 3% CTR will cost you less than one with a 0.8% CTR — for the same audience.

Seasonality: Q4 (October–December) is the most expensive period globally as advertisers compete for Christmas budgets. In South Africa, this coincides with end-of-year business spending spikes. Expect CPMs 30–60% higher in November–December.

Funnel stage: Cold audiences (awareness) are cheaper to reach. Retargeting warm audiences (website visitors, video viewers) costs more but converts better.

What Budget Do You Need?

For Meta ads to work in South Africa, you need enough budget for Meta's algorithm to exit the learning phase — typically 50 conversion events per week per ad set. Below this, delivery becomes unstable and costs spike.

For a lead generation campaign targeting a Johannesburg audience: a minimum of R5,000–R8,000 per month in ad spend to gather meaningful data. For e-commerce with pixel-based optimisation: R10,000+ per month to give the algorithm enough signal to optimise. Budget below these thresholds and you'll spend money learning slowly rather than scaling what works.

Meta Advantage+ vs Manual Campaigns: Which Is Cheaper?

Meta's Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns and Advantage+ Audience targeting use AI to find your best customers — often delivering lower CPAs than manually targeted campaigns, particularly for e-commerce. For service businesses, the picture is more mixed: Advantage+ Audience can find unexpected segments, but you give up precise audience control. The best approach is usually to test both and let data decide.
