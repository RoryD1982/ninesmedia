Landing Page Optimisation in South Africa: Turn More Clicks Into Leads

By Brandon Howard 2026-10-06 7 min read

You can have the best Google Ads campaign in the country — perfectly targeted keywords, compelling copy, aggressive bidding — and still lose money if your landing page doesn't convert. Most South African businesses spend 90% of their marketing budget on getting traffic and 10% on converting it. The ratio should be closer to 70/30.

Conversion rate optimisation (CRO) is the process of improving your landing page so a higher percentage of visitors take action. Improving your conversion rate from 2% to 4% effectively doubles the return on every rand you spend on advertising.

The 5 Most Common Landing Page Mistakes in SA

1. Sending Ad Traffic to Your Homepage

Your homepage is for browsing. A landing page is for converting. If someone clicks an ad for "roof repairs Pretoria" and lands on your homepage, they have to hunt for what they want. Most won't. Send ad traffic to a dedicated page that matches the ad exactly.

2. Too Many Options

Every option on a page that isn't your primary CTA is a distraction. Navigation menus, blog links, social media buttons — these all reduce conversions. A high-converting landing page has one goal and one CTA.

3. The Form Is Too Long

In South Africa, where trust is still being built online, long forms kill conversions. For lead generation, ask for name, phone number, and one qualifying question. Nothing more. You can gather other details once you've made contact.

4. No Social Proof

South Africans are sceptical online buyers. Reviews, testimonials, case study snippets, client logos, and "as featured in" mentions all dramatically improve conversion rates. Place them close to your CTA — not buried at the bottom.

5. Slow Load Speed

A 1-second delay in page load time reduces conversions by 7%. On South Africa's mobile networks — where many users are on 3G/4G — page speed is critical. Use Google's PageSpeed Insights to test yours. Aim for a score above 80 on mobile.

What a High-Converting Landing Page Looks Like

  • Headline: Matches the ad. States the benefit clearly. "Get 3 Solar Quotes in 24 Hours" beats "Welcome to SA Solar Solutions".
  • Subheadline: Backs up the headline with one more compelling detail
  • Hero image or video: Shows the outcome, not the process
  • 3–5 bullet benefits: What they get, not what you offer
  • Form or CTA button: Above the fold on desktop, easy to find on mobile
  • Trust signals: Reviews, logos, certifications — near the CTA
  • Risk reversal: "No obligation", "Free quote", "Cancel anytime"

Testing: The Fastest Way to Improve Conversions

Don't guess what works — test it. Run an A/B test on your headline: keep everything else identical and change only the headline between two versions. Run each version until you have at least 100 conversions, then keep the winner. Repeat with the CTA button, form length, and hero image.

Google Optimize (now integrated into GA4) lets you run free A/B tests. VWO and Hotjar are paid options that add heatmaps and session recordings — invaluable for understanding why visitors aren't converting.
