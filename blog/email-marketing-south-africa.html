Email Marketing in South Africa: Does It Still Work in 2026?

By Brandon Howard 2026-10-05 7 min read

With WhatsApp, TikTok, and Instagram dominating attention in South Africa, many business owners have written off email marketing as outdated. They'd be wrong. Email marketing still delivers the highest ROI of any digital channel — globally and in South Africa — when done right.

The average return is R36 for every R1 spent. The businesses seeing that ROI aren't the ones sending monthly newsletters that nobody reads. They're the ones using email strategically, at the right moment, with the right message.

Email Marketing Stats for South Africa (2026)

  • Average email open rate in SA: 22–28% (vs 3–5% organic reach on Facebook)
  • Average click-through rate: 2.5–4%
  • Email is checked daily by over 70% of South African internet users
  • Promotional emails are still the #1 driver of e-commerce sales in South Africa

What Types of Email Marketing Work Best in SA?

Welcome Sequences

The most valuable emails you'll ever send. When someone signs up or enquires, a 3–5 email automated welcome sequence introduces your brand, builds trust, and moves them toward a sale. These are set up once and run forever.

Promotional Campaigns

Time-sensitive offers, seasonal campaigns, and new service announcements. Works for both B2C (retail, e-commerce) and B2B (professional services, SaaS).

Nurture Sequences

For leads who aren't ready to buy yet. A series of valuable emails over 4–8 weeks that educates and builds trust until they're ready. Particularly effective for higher-ticket services like financial advisory, property, and digital marketing.

Transactional Emails

Order confirmations, receipts, shipping updates — these have 8x the open rate of regular marketing emails. Include a cross-sell or referral ask in every transactional email.

Building an Email List in South Africa

The biggest challenge for most SA businesses isn't sending emails — it's having someone to send them to. Build your list through:

  • A lead magnet on your website (free guide, checklist, discount code)
  • Pop-up forms with a genuine value offer — not just "sign up for updates"
  • Post-purchase opt-in for e-commerce customers
  • Social media promotions driving email sign-ups
  • In-store or in-person sign-up lists digitised via tablet

POPIA and Email Marketing in South Africa

The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) requires that you have consent before sending marketing emails to South African residents. This means:

  • Never buy email lists — this is both illegal under POPIA and ineffective
  • Use double opt-in where possible
  • Include an easy unsubscribe link in every email
  • Store only what you need and have a privacy policy on your website

Is Email Marketing Worth It for Your Business?

If you have a customer list and you're not emailing them regularly, you're leaving money on the table. Even a basic monthly newsletter to 500 engaged subscribers will outperform a cold audience campaign on most metrics. Start simple — one email per month — and build from there.
