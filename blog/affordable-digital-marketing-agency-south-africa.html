Affordable Digital Marketing Agency in South Africa

"Affordable" shouldn't mean cheap work. It should mean a plan sized to your budget: putting your money into the one or two channels most likely to bring customers, setting them up properly, and not paying for activity that doesn't move your numbers. That's how we approach smaller budgets.

Who This Suits

  • Small and medium businesses taking their first serious step into digital marketing, or replacing ad hoc DIY campaigns.
  • Service businesses that need a steady flow of calls and enquiries — professional services, trades, healthcare practices, property, insurance and education.
  • Startups and new brands that need to prove a channel works before spending more.
  • Businesses burned by agencies before — unclear reports, lock-in contracts or no access to their own accounts.

It's probably not the right fit if you need a large creative campaign across many channels at once, or if your total budget, including ad spend, is under about R8,000 a month. In that case, start with your Google Business Profile and local SEO, which cost nothing but time.

When the first channel is working, the Growth package (from R12,000/month) adds a second paid channel, social media on two platforms, landing page optimisation and weekly reporting.

Management Fees vs Ad Spend

Your total monthly cost has two separate parts:

  • Management fee — paid to Nines Media for strategy, setup, optimisation, creative and reporting. Fixed, not a percentage of your spend.
  • Ad spend — paid directly to Google or Meta, from ad accounts in your name. You control it, you can see every rand, and it never passes through us.

Keeping them separate means you always know what you're paying for, and our fee doesn't grow just because you spend more on ads.

How We Keep It Affordable Without Cutting Corners

  • Focus. We start with the one channel most likely to bring you customers instead of spreading a small budget across five.
  • No lock-in. Month-to-month packages, so you're never paying for a contract you've outgrown.
  • No padding. The scope only includes work that moves your numbers.
  • Your accounts, your data. If you ever leave, your campaigns and history stay with you.
  • Fix the leaks first. We check tracking, landing pages and lead follow-up before asking you to spend more on ads.

What to Expect in the First 90 Days

  1. Weeks 1–2: free audit, agreed targets, tracking and campaign setup.
  2. Weeks 3–6: campaigns live; the platforms learn which searches and audiences convert. Enquiries usually start in this period.
  3. Months 2–3: cost per lead typically improves as we cut waste and scale what works. Then we decide together whether to add a channel.

We won't promise rankings or guaranteed lead numbers — nobody honestly can. We will show you exactly what your money produced each month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest way to get professional digital marketing in South Africa?

Start with one well-managed paid channel and a fully optimised Google Business Profile. Nines Media's Starter package covers one channel from R5,000/month in management, with ad spend paid separately to Google or Meta.

How much does an affordable digital marketing agency cost?

Packages start from R5,000/month; final scope is quoted individually. With typical ad spend of R3,000 to R10,000, most small businesses budget R8,000 to R15,000 a month in total.

Is ad spend included in the R5,000?

No. The R5,000 is the management fee. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta from accounts you own, so you can see exactly where every rand goes.

Is there a contract?

No. All packages are month-to-month.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. Most clients start with one channel, then add a second channel, social media or SEO once the first is producing customers profitably.

What if my budget is smaller than R8,000 a month in total?

Focus on free foundations first: a complete Google Business Profile, reviews and a clear website. Our free audit will tell you honestly whether paid advertising makes sense yet.
