Pricing FAQs

Does ad spend come out of the monthly fee?

No. Your ad budget goes directly to Google, Meta or LinkedIn — you pay the platforms directly, or we invoice it separately and pass it through at cost with zero markup. Our fee covers strategy, campaign management and creative only.

Is there a minimum contract period?

No lock-in. All packages are month-to-month. We ask for 30 days' notice to wind down campaigns cleanly, but there are no penalties for leaving.

What ad spend should I budget alongside the management fee?

For most SMEs in South Africa, a minimum of R3 000–R5 000/month in ad spend per channel delivers meaningful results. We'll recommend a budget based on your goals and category during the free audit.

Can I start small and scale up?

Absolutely. Many of our clients start on the Starter package and move to Growth once they see traction. We'll advise when the time is right — not before.
