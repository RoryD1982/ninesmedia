Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

How much does Nines Media charge?

Packages start from R5,000 per month (Starter), R12,000 per month (Growth) and R25,000 per month (Full Service). Websites start from R15,000 and brand identity packages from R8,000. Every engagement is quoted individually after a free audit.

Why do you quote each job instead of publishing fixed prices?

Because the right channels, creative volume and ad budget differ for every business. The from-prices show where each package starts; your quote reflects the work your goals actually need, with no padding.

Is ad spend included in your packages?

No. Ad spend is billed separately and paid directly to Google, Meta or LinkedIn from accounts you own. Our fees cover strategy, management, creative and reporting.

Are there lock-in contracts?

No. Packages are month-to-month. We earn the next month by delivering results.

What does the free audit include?

A senior strategist reviews your ads, website, social presence and competitors, then sends a written report with your biggest growth levers, wasted spend and a prioritised action plan, plus a tailored quote.
